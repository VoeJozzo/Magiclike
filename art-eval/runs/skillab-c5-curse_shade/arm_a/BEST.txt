This frame best enacts both halves of Curse Shade's mechanic (a dying Specter that exiles a target creature). On the left, a tall hooded specter with burning violet eyes and a lower body fraying into ribbons of shadow reads instantly as a dying wraith. Its skeletal arm reaches right into a blazing violet-and-white void portal in which a soldier-figure is suspended and visibly dissolving — particles stream off the top of the orb as he is unmade, which depicts EXILE (banished from existence), not mere death. It carries clean black-card color identity (violet/obsidian energy over a muted dark-teal graveyard), fills the frame as a scene rather than a sprite, and harmonizes with the set's void-energy vocabulary (doom_blade). It is the converged result of the gen 03 -> 05 -> 07 seed-locked line: gen 07 held the strong gen 05 composition while intensifying the dissolution/particle effect, making the unmaking the most legible of all rolls. The only minor flaw is the victim figure being slightly small/dark inside the bright orb — a fixable specific flaw, not generic emptiness.
