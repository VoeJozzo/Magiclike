This frame enacts Drain Life's mechanic completely and legibly: a central black-robed sorcerer cups two orbs of stolen green life-essence in his raised hands while a withering green beast (the "creature" target) on the left and a slumped armored soldier (the "opp" target) on the right are visibly drained, both fully visible, distinct, and the same size. The two-victims-into-one-thief composition mirrors the card exactly (2 damage to a creature + 2 to the opponent, 4 life flowing back to the caster), and the green essence reading as life leaving the victims and pooling in the sorcerer gives the "gain life" beat its literal picture. Color identity is solidly black with the sickly-green drain glow doing the work, and the three-point silhouette reads cleanly at 64x32. It is a seed-locked refinement of gen 3 that fixed that frame's only weakness — victim legibility — while keeping its balanced composition; the alternate convergent-V direction (gens 4/6) had stronger energy flow but never got both victims to read as a distinct creature-and-person, so gen 5 wins on faithful mechanic enactment.
