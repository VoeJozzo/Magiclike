This frame enacts all three of the card's types at once with a single legible silhouette. Verdant Verge is an Artifact Land Forest that produces green mana, and its name ("Verge" = boundary/threshold/edge) points at the place where the wild forest begins. The art shows an ornate carved stone archway — wrought and keystoned, so it reads as a built ARTIFACT, not just natural rock — moss-slicked and draped in deep emerald vines, standing as the threshold (the "verge") between a dirt path and dense old-growth woodland. A bold vertical pillar of bright lime-green mana light glows within the opening, making the LAND's green mana production the visible hero element. Framed by dark tree trunks and canopy with a path passing through, it is a deep-forest SCENE rather than a sprite on negative space, and its color identity is unambiguously green.

It was reached by diagnose-then-iterate: gen 1 established the overgrown-archway composition but the mana glow never materialized (the named flaw); a seed-locked tweak (gen 3) added the glow; a final seed-locked refinement (gen 5) sharpened the arch into a symmetrical, ornate, keystoned structure and made the green mana-light a bold central pillar. It beats gen 4 (a cleaner but timid archway with no mana glow at all — it read as plain ruins) precisely because it reaches for and lands the full mechanic.
