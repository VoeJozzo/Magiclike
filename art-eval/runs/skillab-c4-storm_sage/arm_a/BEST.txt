This is the only roll that enacts Storm Sage's actual trigger rather than just its theme. Her ability draws a card whenever another creature you control enters the battlefield, and this frame literally stages that causal moment: a freshly-summoned armored knight on the left, an arc of cerulean Lightning leaping from him into the open spellbook held by the blue-hooded sage on the right — the ally's arrival feeding knowledge (the draw) straight to her book. Both figures are fully visible and on-palette (steel-plate ally reads as a summoned creature, not a second wizard — fixing gen 04's purple-mage problem), the blue color identity is unmistakable, and the silhouette is clean: two figures bridged by a bright bolt. Gen 01 is the runner-up — a gorgeous single-figure storm-scholar with a decisive bolt from the book — but it only depicts the storm/sage flavor, not the entering-creature mechanic. Mechanic-in-art is the deciding principle, so gen 05 wins despite the central bolt blending slightly with the knight's sword.
