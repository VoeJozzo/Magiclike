This frame best enacts the card's mechanic AND nails its color identity. Exorcist is a white Human Cleric whose ETB exiles a target creature (the controller gains life equal to its power) — a merciful banishment, not a kill. The art shows exactly that: a cleric in cream-white-and-gold hooded robes stands in clean side profile, hand thrust out, channeling a column of holy white-gold light that strikes a dark-red horned creature on the right, whose lower body is breaking apart into rising golden motes as it is driven out of existence. The exile reads (creature dissolving into light, going elsewhere) and the warm gold glow stands in for the life released. Of all six rolls it has the strongest white-set color discipline (the cream/gold cleric — gens 3 and 6 drifted grey), a legible two-figure silhouette with the holy figure vs. the banished creature in clear opposition, and a framed stone-chapel background that keeps it a scene rather than a sprite on negative space. Gen 3 (deeper chapel, more dramatic light column) and gen 6 (more dynamic demonic creature) are strong alternates but lose on robe color.
