Best enactment of the Vile Edict mechanic (opponent is forced to choose and sacrifice one of their own permanents). The frame shows two clearly-lit, fully-visible figures: a hooded black-robed lord on the left, arm fully extended and finger pointing in cruel condemnation, and a kneeling armored soldier on the right being consumed by a wine-purple/obsidian death vortex. Crucially, a bright crackling arc of violet death energy leaps from the lord's fingertip across the gap to the victim, so cause (the condemnation) and effect (the dying man) are linked in a single read -- the art literally shows one figure condemning his own man to die, which is exactly what distinguishes an edict from ordinary removal. It is the most cinematic frame in the set while remaining the most legible: pure black/violet color identity, strong silhouette (condemner + arc + dying victim), both subjects readable at 64x32. It is the convergence of a seed-locked iteration line (gen 06 -> 07) that fixed the one weakness of the earlier leaders (figures too dark, gesture ambiguous) without drifting off the composition.
