This frame best enacts City Guardian's mechanic: a lone legendary soldier standing dead-center in a tall stone city gateway, planted halberd at his side, bodily blocking the only way through. That gatekeeper-at-the-threshold image is the visual of the card's defining rule — "All cards cost {1} more to cast" reads as a sentinel who taxes everyone trying to pass. The white-silver plate with gold trim carries the {W} color identity, the pale granite gate and iron-banded doors carry the colorless half, the crimson cape gives the legendary figure a singular focal presence, and the alert weapon-forward stance suits First Strike. Among the strong cluster (gens 2/5/7, all seed-locked on the same gateway composition) this one wins on legibility: the halberd reads as a crisp vertical planted polearm rather than an awkward diagonal, the heavy timber gate pillars frame him tightly to reinforce "blocking the way," and the gold breastplate studs hold up at 64x32. The mechanic, the colors, and the legendary presence all land in one readable silhouette.
