This frame most completely enacts the card's mechanic: Brand of Iron is a red sorcery that permanently turns a target creature into an Artifact. The boar is shown caught mid-transformation in clean side profile -- bristly brown living flesh in front, solid riveted iron plate behind -- with a bright molten-orange brand seam searing vertically at the exact boundary where flesh becomes metal. You can read the whole rule at a glance: the brand (red, the card's color) is applied, and the creature is becoming iron (an artifact). The silhouette is legible at 64x32, the red color identity comes through the glowing seam and forge coals, and it matches the card's name literally. It beats gen 04 (same concept but no brand seam -- the transformation read but the "brand" act did not) and gen 06 (atmospheric forge arch, but the metal read as scaly hide rather than riveted iron, weakening the artifact-type read). The seed-locked tweak from gen 04 added exactly the one element it was missing without disturbing the composition.
