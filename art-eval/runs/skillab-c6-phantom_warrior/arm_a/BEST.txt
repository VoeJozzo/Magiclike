Cleanest, most legible frame at 64x32 and the strongest gestalt of the run. A glowing pale-cyan spectral warrior strides forward, sword leading, while a living defender braces a round shield and recoils under a large moon. It stages the card's identity in a single readable beat -- an unstoppable Spirit warrior advancing on a defender whose block (the shield) is about to fail -- which is exactly what "unblockable" means in-scene. The phantom is the best-rendered, most readable spectral figure of the set, matching echo_spirit / vengeful_spirit's spectral-blue vocabulary and the set's moody night palette. Its one fixable flaw is a small gap between the spirit and the shield (phase-through is implied, not literal); per the diagnosable-flaw principle I prefer this reaching, instantly-readable confrontation over the timider alternatives. Gen 6 (spirit at a stone-wall archway) had the more literal "ignores the fortification" idea but reads as standing-in-a-doorway rather than passing through stone; gen 8 is detailed but busier with the same implied gap.
