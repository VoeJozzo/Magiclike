This frame reads instantly and correctly as the card: a flying aristocratic vampire lord, vast wine-red leathery bat wings spread wide, hovering over a moonlit gothic manor with a lit tower and a full moon haloing his wings. Flying is Sengir's most literal must-depict keyword (a grounded flyer reads wrong), and this is the airborne set's strongest, most balanced gothic scene with a clean, dominant winged silhouette at 64x32 and full black/wine color identity. A drained shape hangs beneath his grip, gesturing at the lifelink/feed-and-grow predation that defines the card. I weighed gen_04 (a beautifully legible throat-bite life-drain that nails the lifelink/grow-on-kill mechanic) as the runner-up, but it is fully grounded and drops flying entirely, which the art discipline flags as the costlier miss for a flyer. Gen_03 and gen_07 are near-twins in quality; gen_07 wins on the richer background (manor plus tower plus moon) and a marginally more dynamic flight pose.
