Alloy Construct is a colorless 2/2 artifact creature with no keywords — a literal card whose "mechanic" is its own nature: an ALLOY (multiple fused metals) CONSTRUCT (a fabricated body). Gen 7 enacts exactly that. The figure is patchworked from visibly distinct metals — golden-brass chest, green verdigris joints, copper accents, dark iron limbs with welded seams and bright metallic highlights — so "alloy" reads at a glance, and the blank faceless iron helm makes it unmistakably a built construct rather than an organic beast (the failure mode seen in gen 5). It holds a strong, blocky hulking silhouette at 64x32, and the dim brick foundry with the forge-glow at left grounds it as a scene rather than a sprite floating on negative space. It is the cleanest construct-read within the best (most dynamic, asymmetric) composition family, edging out gen 3 (same seed/composition, but a more face-like head) and the more frontal/static gen 6.
