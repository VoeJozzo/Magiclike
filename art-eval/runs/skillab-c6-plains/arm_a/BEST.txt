This is the strongest "Plains" of the run. A basic land is a place, and its only mechanic is "tap for white mana," so the art should depict the white-aligned terrain that is the source of that mana. This frame does it best: a gleaming white stone chapel framed by a green tree on the left, a warm golden grass-field sweeping to the right, and a winding dirt path leading toward the chapel under a beautifully detailed blue sky with fluffy white clouds. The white chapel carries white's color identity and its order/civilization pillar unmistakably (white building + blue sky + gold field = the classic Plains palette), so the frame reads as a *white* land rather than just any sunlit meadow. The dirt path gives the eye a route into the scene and adds depth the pure-landscape rolls lacked. It is crisp pixel art consistent with the set's style, has a strong, legible silhouette at 64x32 (chapel + tree against the horizon/sky split), and was produced as a seed-locked tweak of gen 5 that held the composition while gaining the path. Close runners-up: gen 5 (same scene without the path) and gen 8 (a purer all-white spired chapel with a dramatic cloud); gen 1 was the best pure sun-over-field landscape if a landmark-free vista were preferred.
