Rationale: Best enacts the card's mechanic — a Land entering play / ramp. A
broad burst of fresh emerald grass and saplings erupts upward through splitting
brown soil with clods of dirt flung up, against a dim old-growth forest. It
reads unmistakably as NEW land violently growing into being across a clearing
(not a pre-existing tree or a single sprite), which is exactly what Rampant
Growth does. Strong central silhouette (a green eruption from a dark earth
baseline), correct green color identity, and it sits naturally in the set's
forested-scene vocabulary (ancient_treant, choking_vines).

Runner-up: rampant_growth_gen_07_seed1770130322.png — a striking macro of a seed
splitting with a sprout exploding out, dew and dirt suspended; the most
beautiful single frame, but reads as one sprout rather than terrain/land, and
its bokeh background is slightly sprite-like for the set.
