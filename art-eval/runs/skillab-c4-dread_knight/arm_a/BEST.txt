Dread Knight's defining mechanic is its enter-the-battlefield cost: summoning it makes you lose 2 life. This frame is the one that actually ENACTS that blood-price rather than just decorating the theme. A towering knight in soot-black plate with a tattered crimson cape stands in a menacing crypt arch, and crimson life-essence is visibly siphoning down his form and pooling in a bright blood-red glow at his feet and across the ground — the room bleeding into him, the life-drain made literal. The silhouette reads cleanly and unmistakably at 64x32 as a single commanding dread-knight figure (menace = unstoppable, fearsome presence), and the color identity is dead-on for a heavy-black creature: black armor, crimson light, no off-color wash. It was reached via a seed-locked tweak of gen 01 that intensified the crimson siphon, which is exactly what pushed the mechanic from ambient red lighting (gen 04) to a visible, dramatic life-drain. Gen 04 has a crisper sword and a more groomed frontal figure, but its drain is only mood lighting; per the discipline of favoring the frame that reaches for the real mechanic over the safer, prettier one, gen 03 is the pick.
