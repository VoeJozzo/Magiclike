This roll most legibly enacts the card's mechanic — a Wizard whose job is countering enemy spells. A composed, hooded blue wizard holds up a crisp cerulean hexagonal ward, and a hostile red spell is visibly shattering against it, scattering crimson shards off the right edge. The blue-vs-red color contrast reads instantly as "blue counters red," which is exactly what Counter Specialist does (it grows whenever you cast a counter spell). The defensive, unbothered, hand-raised pose suits the 1/4 body (durable, denying, not attacking), the blue color identity is clean and dominant, the stone-pillared hall gives it real scene depth (not a sprite on negative space), and the silhouette — figure plus glowing hexagon — stays readable at 64x32. It was reached by seed-locking the strong Gen 4 interception composition and tweaking the ward to a hexagon and sharpening the enemy-spell contrast; the composition held and the mechanic read improved. Gen 3 is the best of the alternate "unravelling a red glyph" direction and is the runner-up.
