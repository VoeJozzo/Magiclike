Gen 09 is the strongest frame across the full run. The composition fully enacts the Inferno Caller's trigger mechanic: the shaman is mid battle-lunge (attacking), and a crimson fireball with a white-hot core is visibly streaking rightward from their outstretched palm toward an unseen target — the damage fires simultaneously with the attack, and both are present in the same frame. The fireball is the brightest object, drawing the eye to the mechanic rather than the figure alone. The silhouette at 64x32 is clear and readable: a running humanoid shape with a projectile exiting right. The volcanic dusk backdrop (orange sky, black craggy spires) anchors the card's red color identity without relying on lazy sky-fire clichés — it's a scene, not atmosphere. Compared to gen 01 (same seed, slightly softer prompt), gen 09 sharpened the fireball prominence and made the craggy background more distinct; compared to gen 04 (victim POV), gen 09 keeps the caller as protagonist — the right choice for a creature card.
