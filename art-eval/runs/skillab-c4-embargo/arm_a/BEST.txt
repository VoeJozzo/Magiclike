Embargo bounces a creature to hand AND stamps it with a stackable cost-increase sticker; this frame is the only one that enacts BOTH halves cleanly and legibly. The gilded, red-caped customs official at right raises a brilliant white-gold heraldic seal-stamp disc — an unmistakable, iconic "duty/tax brand," directly visualizing the cost_mod sticker — while a taut golden chain runs from that seal to a dark horned beast at lower-left that is plainly being hauled backward off the field, head down and resisting (the bounce-to-hand). The silhouette reads instantly at 64x32: bright seal disc upper-center, dark dragged beast lower-left, armored authority figure right, framed by stone toll-gate pillars. Color identity is correct for a white/colorless card (white-gold radiance, gilded plate). It beats Gen 01 on the creature's clearly-dragged posture, and Gen 04/05 on the size, crispness, and white-gold purity of the hero seal element. A reaching, specific, mechanic-true frame rather than a generic bounce.
