This frame lands the card's mechanic most legibly with zero knowledge of intent. Symbiote Tree's whole identity is a treefolk that enters and bestows +1/+1 plus Reach on one of YOUR creatures -- a symbiotic gift, not a tree standing alone. Gen 8 enacts exactly that: a clear treefolk on the left, a bold unmistakable lime-green sap-rope arcing across the frame into a large, clearly canine gray wolf on the right that is visibly muscular and empowered. Both halves of the rule read on the canvas -- the +1/+1 growth (the big powerful wolf) and the symbiotic energy transfer (the bright green bridge literally connecting tree to ally). It has the cleanest two-mass silhouette of the set (bark mass / glowing arc / big wolf), strong green color identity, and matches the forest-scene aesthetic of the anchors (ancient_treant, wolfbriar_elemental). Gen 6 was more polished but the buff didn't read; gen 5 had the biggest buff but an ambiguous boar-like beast; gen 4 had the best treefolk face but the subtlest transfer. Gen 8 best satisfies the mechanic-enactment-first test, which is the top axis.
