This frame is the clearest enactment of "Strength of the Pack" (a green +2/+2 + trample combat trick): a single powerful lead wolf surging forward with the full strength of its pack charging close behind it. The size contrast reads immediately — the alpha is visibly larger and more muscular than the pack (the +2/+2 buff made literal), while the dense wall of wolves driving up behind it is the "pack" the card names as the source of that strength. The lead wolf's head is crisp and readable with a snarling face, the body is well-defined in forward motion, and the kicked-up snow plus the cold blue old-growth pine night give it a true scene rather than a sprite-on-negative-space. Color identity is solidly green/natural (forest, snow, earthy wolf tones). It beat the trample/overrun-the-elk concept (gen 2), which fell to the placid-prey subject prior — the elk stood calmly instead of being bowled over. Gen 5 was a near-tie striding variant, but gen 4 has the most dynamic forward read and the cleanest alpha silhouette.
