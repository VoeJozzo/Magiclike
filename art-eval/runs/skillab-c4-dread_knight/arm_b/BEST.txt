This frame enacts the card's mechanics most completely and reads cleanest at 64x32. The subject is unmistakably a Dread Knight: a towering figure in blackened, pitted plate with a horned skull-faced helm and a tattered crimson cape, carrying the card's black mana identity in the dark armor and the crimson accents. The massive two-handed greatsword planted point-down before him gives the imposing, fearsome silhouette that sells Menace (a threat one defender can't hold). Most importantly, the buried blade glows crimson like lifeblood drawn into the earth, which enacts the enter-the-battlefield "you lose 2 life" trigger -- this knight's arrival is paid in blood. The pale-moon halo and flanking graveyard crosses ground it as a black-magic necropolis summoning. Among the six rolls it has the strongest gestalt, the clearest readable weapon, and the most direct mechanic-in-art mapping; the dynamic gen 5 was a close runner-up but busier and less iconic.
