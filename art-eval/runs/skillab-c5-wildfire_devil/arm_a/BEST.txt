Wildfire Devil's defining mechanic is that its DEATH is the payoff: when it dies it deals 3 damage to any target. Gen 5 is the only frame that fully enacts that rule rather than decorating the theme. On the left stands an unmistakable upright horned demon with a charred black skeletal frame and a glowing molten chest — a clean, legible body-type read for a 3/3 Demon at 64x32. On the right, a victim is fully engulfed in flame and recoiling, clearly being burned. The two-figure beat lands: the demon's fire is striking a target, exactly mirroring "deals 3 damage to a creature or player." Both silhouettes are distinct and fully visible, lit only by the fire, against a coherent scorched night battlefield. Its one fixable weakness — the molten torso glow sitting slightly low — is the kind of minor flaw the skill says not to down-rank a reaching frame for. Gen 7 (its seed-locked twin) and the iconic Gen 6 detonation portrait are strong runners-up, but Gen 6 only implies the target and Gen 5's demon silhouette is the crispest of the set while still showing the victim being hit.
