Best enacts the mechanic: a wizard duel where a crimson mage's fireball collides with a blue sorcerer's counter-magic at a bright white flashpoint dead center, the fire visibly scattering into sparks as it is snuffed. The act of countering IS the focal point of the frame, not decoration. Two clear figures, classic pointy-hat wizard silhouettes, a clean warm-vs-cool color split that carries the card's mono-blue identity against an opposing red spell. The whole story (a spell being stopped mid-cast) reads in one glance even at 64x32, and the flash-keyword "instant reaction" feel is implicit in the mid-air interception. Gen 1 is the same vision and nearly as good; gen 7 is the cleanest pure shatter-against-a-rune-shield but lacks a human element. Gen 6 wins on both legibility of the mechanic and silhouette strength at card size.
