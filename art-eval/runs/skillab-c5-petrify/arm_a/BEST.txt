Petrify turns a creature into an Artifact until end of turn — the in-world reading is a living beast turned to stone, an inanimate object/monument. This frame nails that mechanic-enactment: a snarling wolf rendered as a faceted carved-stone statue standing on a chiseled stone pedestal in a dim forest. The plinth is the load-bearing tell — it reads instantly as "statue / inert art-object," which is exactly the creature-becomes-Artifact transformation, far more legibly than the half-flesh/half-stone boundary attempts (gens 1-3, where the flesh-to-stone gradient never rendered cleanly at 64x32). Against the other strong statue contenders (gen 7, gen 8), gen 5 has the best figure-ground contrast and the most dynamic, readable prowling silhouette; gen 7's pose is flatter and gen 8 blends into the misty background. The faint cool white glow carries the white-magic color identity of a {W} spell.
