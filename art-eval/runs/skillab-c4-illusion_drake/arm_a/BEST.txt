Gen 09 is the strongest frame across the ten generations. The worm's-eye view with the drake's bat-wings fully spread against a bright moon delivers the single most legible dragon silhouette possible at 64x32 — the symmetrical wingspan fills the frame and reads as "dragon" in one glance. The seed-locked tweak added "stained glass" wing veining, which directly enacts the "Illusion" type: the wings are translucent cerulean panels divided by dark lines, like light through colored glass, making the phantom quality visible rather than just gestured at. The moonlit backlight glowing through the spectral form reinforces the creature's insubstantial nature. Color identity is pure blue (cerulean, sapphire, ice-blue, indigo sky) with no color contamination. Compared to gen 05 (the banking dive, also strong), gen 09 is the better choice at this pixel count because the spread-wing overhead view is a simpler, more immediately iconic silhouette, while the banking pose requires more pixels to read cleanly.
