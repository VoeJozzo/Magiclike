Predate is a green fight spell: pump your creature +1/+1, then have it fight an opponent's creature. The card's name frames that fight as predation, so the art should show one beast overpowering and killing another, not two creatures squaring off politely. This frame enacts exactly that: a massive green-furred predator looms low over a pale tan-brown deer collapsing beneath it, crimson blood pooling in the foreground, the prey clearly buckling and dying. The two animals read as distinct things (green aggressor vs. tan victim — the forced color contrast that kept the second subject from being swallowed), the predator is unmistakably winning (the +1/+1 edge), and the kill is visceral rather than static. The horizontal predator-over-prey silhouette stays legible at 64x32, and the dim old-growth forest with its green light shaft anchors it to the green-creature set (feralStalker / wolfbriarElemental) while keeping it a real scene, not a sprite on negative space. Gen 5 showed the emerald +1/+1 glow more explicitly but its kill action was softer; Gen 6 wins on the core mechanic-in-art read: a predator killing what it pounced on.
