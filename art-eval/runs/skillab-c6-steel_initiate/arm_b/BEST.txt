This is the only frame that lands all three things the card demands at once. Steel Initiate is a {W} 2/2 Human Soldier whose sole trigger is "when this attacks, gain 1 life" — so the art has to read as (1) a steel-armored soldier, (2) moving into the attack, and (3) gaining life through that aggression. Gen 8 shows a young soldier in clearly legible steel plate striding forward through his own ranks, his entire body wrapped in a brilliant white-and-gold radiant aura of holy healing light that pours down to the ground. The heal is body-centered and unmistakable, and crucially it renders as white-gold — preserving white's color identity — rather than the cyan that crept into gen 5 or the sword-tip "strike" spark that swallowed the heal in gens 6, 7, and 9 (the sword-thrust pose kept relocating the glow to the blade). It enacts the mechanic literally: the act of advancing to attack visibly restores him. It is also the most set-coherent with the existing white-soldier arts (flanked by fellow soldiers, banners, battlefield) and is the most striking, readable silhouette of the spread — a haloed steel figure against a dark line of troops. Runners-up: gen 1 (best raw charge energy, but heal only implied) and gen 4 (best youthful "initiate" character read, but no visible heal).
