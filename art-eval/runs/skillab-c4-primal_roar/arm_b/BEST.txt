Primal Roar is a green sorcery that pumps ALL your creatures +2/+2, so the art has to enact a team-wide surge, not a solo subject. Gen 03 lands that best: a massive alpha dire wolf rears mid-lunge with jaws wide in a thunderous roar (the "roar" made literal), two antlered beasts flank and surge in response (the pack/"all your creatures" scope), and a vivid lime-and-emerald primal-energy glow washes up over the whole group as the scene's light source — the +2/+2 buff shown as visible power rippling through the pack from the roar. It is the most dynamic and most readable of the rolls at 64x32: clear rearing-wolf silhouette, strong green color identity, and all three load-bearing signals (the roar, the team scope, the buff aura) present at once. It came from a seed-locked tweak of gen 01 that held that frame's strong lunging composition while pushing the green energy from ambient forest light into a genuine buff aura — exactly the element the earlier rolls were missing.
