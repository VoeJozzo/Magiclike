Wind Drake is a vanilla 2/2 blue Dragon, so this is a literal-card: the art's job is a small blue aerial dragon that reads instantly at 64x32, airborne (the strongest cross-game convention for a drake), in blue color identity, set in a real sky scene rather than a sprite on negative space. Gen 3 nails all of it. It depicts a sapphire-and-pale-blue drake clearly mid-flight — wings raised and fully spread, body angled in a gliding dive with legs tucked, long tail streaming — soaring over a bank of white cumulus against a cerulean sky. The silhouette is the cleanest, most unmistakable "blue dragon in flight" of the four rolls; the raised-wing glide gives it the wind-riding energy the name "Wind Drake" wants, and it fixed the slightly grounded read of its seed-parent (gen 2) via a seed-locked pose tweak. Color identity, mechanic-appropriate flying depiction, and overall gestalt legibility all land together.
