This frame is the fullest enactment of the card's mechanic: Healing Light restores 5 life, and here the light literally IS the healing -- a brilliant radiant silvery-white bloom bursts from the kneeling cleric's hands with crepuscular rays, washing over a wounded soldier lying prone beneath her. Both figures read instantly and cleanly at 64x32 (the two-figure silhouette holds at gestalt), the white color identity is dead-on (cool silvery-white, having diagnosed and avoided the "golden" -> fire-orange trap that wrecked gens 1, 2, and 8), and the glow is the unmistakable hero element rather than mere decoration. It harmonizes with the set's intimate white-healing vocabulary (Healing Salve's soft-glow-as-only-light, Dawn Angel's radiant figure) while distinguishing itself from Healing Salve by making the light -- not a potion -- the source. Chosen over gen 5 (same validated prompt, but a smaller, more modest bloom) because it reaches harder: the light is dominant and dramatic, which is exactly what a card named "Healing Light" wants.
