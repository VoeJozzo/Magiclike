Rationale: Strongest mechanic-in-art read of the batch. Two clearly distinct figures
enact the drain: a hooded black-robed sorcerer at left, hands raised and pulling, and a
collapsing victim sprawled on the crypt floor at right with a brilliant column of sickly
violet life-essence being torn straight up out of his body. The victim's collapsing pose
sells "life being ripped out" more literally than any other frame, while the violet
essence is the sole light source (matching the card's color identity, B + colorless).
Strong, legible silhouette at 64x32 (robed figure / bright vertical siphon / prone body),
and it harmonizes tonally with the black anchors doom_blade, soul_reaper, and
consume_spirit (dark interior, intimate violence, energy-as-light). Close runners-up:
gen_07 (clean upright two-figure siphon) and gen_02 (bright hand-to-victim transfer).
