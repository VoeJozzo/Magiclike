This frame is the only one where Echo Spirit's mechanic actually reads. The card is a flying Spirit whose death-trigger bounces a creature, so the art has to show one being banishing another. Here the winged, translucent cerulean spirit hovers airborne on the left (satisfying the flying literal-depiction convention), its outstretched hand emitting a visible stream of glowing blue motes toward an armored knight on the right who is visibly breaking apart and dissolving into that same swirl of particles — the bounce is enacted, not merely decorated. The two-figure silhouette is clean and legible at 64x32 (airborne ghost + dispersing victim, connected by the mote-stream that tells the story), and the whole image stays in tight blue/sapphire color identity lit only by the spirit's glow. Gens 1, 4, and 5 left the target knight reading as a solid, un-banished figure, so the mechanic didn't land; gen 3 (a seed-locked tweak of gen 2 that pushed the "scatter of motes" wording) is the one that made the banishment visible.
