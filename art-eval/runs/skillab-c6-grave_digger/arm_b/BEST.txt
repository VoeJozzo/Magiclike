Rationale: Best mechanic-in-art read of the batch. Grave Digger's ETB returns a
creature from the graveyard to hand, so the art needs to SHOW the zombie
reclaiming the dead. Gen 8 does exactly that: a green-grey rotting zombie kneels
at the lip of an open grave and drags a limp PALE human corpse up across the
foreground -- head tipped back, body sprawled, clearly a separate, drained-human
form. It clears the skill's "drained flesh color trap": the corpse reads as a
distinct pale thing against the green zombie, not a second zombie. Clean readable
silhouette at 64x32 (crouched figure + horizontal body), headstone + moon anchor
the moonlit-cemetery palette shared by grave_curate / carrion_feeder. The act of
exhumation -- the card's whole point -- is unmistakable.

Runners-up:
- gen_04 (seed 1178164980): strong two-figure grapple, pale human vs green zombie,
  moon centered. Mechanic reads as "seizing the dead." Body is more upright/whole
  than dragged, slightly less "exhumation."
- gen_01 (seed 1061513673): crouched zombie hauling something pale from the dirt;
  great staging but the corpse is muddier/less clearly a human body.
