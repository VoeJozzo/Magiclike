This frame is the most faithful enactment of Counterspell's mechanic: it shows one spell defeating another spell. The blue-robed caster (left, side profile, arm thrust forward) fires a cerulean arcane wave that collides head-on with an incoming crimson-orange fireball, and the enemy spell shatters into a bright burst of scattering embers at the exact point of impact dead-center. It is the active interception that Counterspell literally is — not a generic wizard casting, and not merely a defensive block. Color identity is clean (blue caster) with a strong contrasting warm enemy spell so the eye reads two distinct spells, one unmaking the other. The dim stone wizard-hall background gives it real scene depth, the clash of magic is the sole light source, and the two-energy collision is the single readable shape at 64x32. Runner-up was gen 08 (the cerulean hexagonal rune-ward with the enemy fireball breaking against it), which has a crisper iconic silhouette and a more present caster, but a ward reads slightly more as "protection/block" than the active "counter" beat that gen 01 captures.
