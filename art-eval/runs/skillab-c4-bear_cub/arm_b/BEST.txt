Bear Cub is a vanilla 1/1 green Bear — a literal-card, not a story-card. The whole identity is "small, young, harmless baby bear," so the art's job is to enact that body type, not dress up a fearsome grizzly. This frame nails it: a plump, round-headed cub with oversized ears sitting upright on its haunches, gazing softly at the viewer, framed symmetrically between two old-growth tree trunks with ferns, moss, and dappled canopy light. It reads unmistakably as a *cub* (youth and smallness are visible in the proportions, not just implied), carries strong green color identity, has the cleanest centered silhouette in the set, and sits in a real opaque forest scene rather than floating on negative space. It was a seed-locked tweak of gen 1 that successfully pushed the proportions babyish (rounder head, softer cheeks, plumper body) while holding the composition — the most fully realized version of the cub the run produced.
