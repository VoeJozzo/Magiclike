This frame best enacts both of the card's mechanics in one legible image. The white-bearded, antler-crowned elf druid in emerald-and-wine robes reads unmistakably as an ELDER and grove patriarch (the static +1/+1 druid-lord identity), while his raised hand conjures a large, clearly-magical swirling spiral of bright lime-green mana energy (the tap-for-GG ability). The figure faces the viewer with a strong, readable silhouette and a clear hero element (the green mana swirl), the color identity is pure green, and the composition is clean at 64x32. It fixed the "candlestick / objects" misread that the twin-column explores (gen 1, 2, 4) suffered from — here the green is conjured magic, not props. Strongest gestalt and mechanic-in-art fusion of the run.
