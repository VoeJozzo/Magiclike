This frame best enacts Embargo's mechanic rather than decorating its theme. The card bounces a creature to its owner's hand AND stamps a stackable cost-surcharge on it; the art fuses both: a white-and-gold customs official throws a hard palm-forward HALT gesture, denying entry to a hulking dark beast that is braced low and recoiling back over the threshold (the bounce / "sent back the way it came"), while the bright gold wax-seal glyph in the official's hand and the gold coins scattered on the ground make the tariff/levy literal (the cost sticker). It has the cleanest two-actor confrontation silhouette of the set — upright glowing lawkeeper vs. hulking repelled animal — readable even at 64x32, framed by the pale marble gate arch with the lawful white city visible beyond. Color identity is correct for a white+colorless card (white-gold order against cold stone). Compared to gen 4 (more drama but the levy reads as a generic light-wall) and gen 5 (best scale/gate framing but the halt gesture and toll-seal go soft), gen 3 keeps both mechanics legible at once.
