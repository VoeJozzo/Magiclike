This frame is the cleanest mechanic-enactment of Faithless Looting's "loot" action: a crimson-robed scholar mid-throw, hurling a spray of fluttering parchment pages into a roaring bonfire (the discard-two half) while clutching a bundle of rolled scrolls to his chest with his back arm (the draw-two half). Both halves of the card read simultaneously at a glance. It's a seed-locked refinement of gen 02 — the lock held the dynamic pose, strong left-fire/right-figure silhouette, and full red color identity while surgically sharpening the airborne-pages and the clutched bundle, exactly the two elements that make the mechanic legible. The "faithless" theme (taking cards only to burn the rest) is literal and unmistakable, the fire is the sole light source, and the ruined-library background gives the scene real depth rather than a sprite on negative space.
