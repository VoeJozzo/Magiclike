This frame is the most complete fusion of "reads instantly as a white guardian angel" and "enacts Vigilance." Both broad feathered wings are spread and rimmed in dawn gold, so the silhouette is unmistakably an angel (most rolls gave only one wing, which reads weaker at 64x32). The seed-locked tweak from gen 07 — asking for "a single long straight longsword raised high" — produced a crisp, unambiguous raised blade extending to the upper-right corner, where earlier rolls had a murky or polearm-like weapon. That raised, ready sword combined with the alert forward-scanning head is exactly the card's mechanic: she strikes yet never drops her guard (Vigilance). The white-and-gold plate over white robes holds the W color identity, and the fortress on the horizon below with the sunrise sky gives her a guardian's post — a real scene, not a sprite on negative space. It out-points gen 02 (cleaner but single-winged, less obviously "angel") and gen 06 (best symmetry but a passive, ambiguous-weapon pose).
