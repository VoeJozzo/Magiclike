This frame best enacts the card's defining mechanic: Vengeful Spirit's death trigger destroys a creature. Gen 06 is the clearest two-figure confrontation of the set — a glowing blue-white winged spectral revenant on the left reaches its arm toward a distinct dark armored soldier on the right who is visibly withering and being consumed in the spirit's icy glow. That outstretched-reach-to-victim line reads directly as "destroy that creature," so the art shows the rule happening rather than just depicting a generic ghost. Flying, the card's only keyword and the strong literal-depiction exception, is unmistakable thanks to the spectral wings and airborne pose. Color identity is correct (white/spectral blue-white, the only light source), the silhouette is legible at 64x32, and the destructive contact actually lands on a separate, readable victim — exactly the beat the weaker rolls (gen 01/05, where the victim stands apart; gen 03/04, where the victim merged into the energy) failed to nail. It reaches for the specific vengeance-from-beyond-the-grave moment and gets it.
