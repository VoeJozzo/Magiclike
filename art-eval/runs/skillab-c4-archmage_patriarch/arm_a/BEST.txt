This frame fuses both of the card's mechanics into one legible scene. Archmage Patriarch's static buff makes him the elder leader of his Wizards, and his trigger loots a card every time you cast a spell — so the art shows an ancient white-bearded archmage seated on a gilded throne-chair (the "patriarch" presiding) reading from a tall open spellbook, while a bright ribbon of cerulean arcane energy and motes streams upward out of the book (enacting the draw-and-discard loot magic firing). The silhouette reads cleanly at 64x32: hunched elder + throne on the left, the vertical glow column center-right, marble column anchoring the right edge. Color identity is squarely blue (sapphire/gold robes, cerulean glow), the face and beard are crisp, the lighting is motivated entirely by the spellbook's glow, and the regal composition is set-coherent with the other blue-wizard arts. Gen 3 was the close runner-up (same mechanic via a rising energy stream) but gen 5's throne makes the "patriarch/leader" reading unambiguous and its gestalt is cleaner.
