Bone Reliquary is an Artifact Land Swamp that taps for black mana, so the art must read as a man-made object that produces black mana, sitting in a swamp. Gen 03 enacts exactly that: an ornate bone-and-iron reliquary casket on a cracked stone plinth, lid ajar, with a deep violet-purple necromantic glow and black mist actively spilling out and pooling across stagnant black swamp water that reflects it. The "mana source" mechanic is shown literally as energy venting from the artifact. It has the cleanest, most iconic silhouette at 64x32 (a clear ornate box on a base, instantly legible), the strongest black-mana color identity (corrected from gen 01's mistaken pink-red toward proper necrotic violet), and a coherent cypress-bog setting that sells the Swamp type. Gen 06 and 07 were close runners-up (06 had more visible skulls but a busier composition; 07 had the most polished framing but its glow stayed contained inside the box rather than emanating, weakening the mana-source read). Gen 03 wins on the gestalt: it reads unmistakably as a bone reliquary breathing black mana into a swamp.
