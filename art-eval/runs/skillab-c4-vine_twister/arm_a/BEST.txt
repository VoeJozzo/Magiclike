This roll is the clearest enactment of Vine Twister's mechanic: on entering the battlefield it grants trample to one of your creatures. The frame shows the mossy bark treefolk at left lashing a thick glowing-green vine across the scene to a charging horned beast at right, which is bearing down on a wooden fence/palisade at the far edge. That reads as the whole mechanic in one image — the Twister visibly transferring force (the vine = the grant) to an ally that is about to break through a barrier (trample = crashing through). It also wins on the craft tests: two distinct, fully-visible subjects at 64x32 (the two-subject scenes in gens 2 and 5 collapsed into a single mass), a clean connecting-arc silhouette, and the strongest green color identity of the batch. Gen 4 had a more dynamic energy-whip but a vaguer right-side creature and no barrier; gen 6's charging quadruped plus visible fence make the break-through beat unambiguous. Seed-locked tweaks (gens 3, 5) consistently degraded the two-subject clarity, so this fresh explore is the keeper.
