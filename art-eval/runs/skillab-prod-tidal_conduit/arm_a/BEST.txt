Rationale: Best fusion of the card's two halves (Artifact + Island, blue) AND
the strongest mechanic-in-art read in the batch. A weathered verdigris-bronze
ring-conduit set into a dark wet sea-cliff visibly DISGORGES glowing cerulean
tidewater into a foaming sea -- the artifact is literally channeling the tide,
which is exactly what a Tidal Conduit land does (a built mechanism converting
seawater into blue mana). Crisp, legible silhouette at 64x32 (the bright
glowing pour against dark rock and the verdigris ring carry the whole frame),
textbook blue color identity, and strong set coherence with the existing
`island` anchor (misty grey-blue sea, low horizon, cool palette).

Runner-up: gen_07 (seed 1932852307) -- a brass tidal-engine with a spoked
verdigris wheel; reads most explicitly as a *machine*/artifact but is busier
and slightly muddier at thumbnail size, so gen_01 wins on gestalt legibility.
Also notable: gen_03 (island shrine waterfall) and gen_05 (tranquil island
vista) for set coherence, gen_06 (monumental tidal gate) for scale.
