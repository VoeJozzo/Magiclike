Alloy Construct is a vanilla 2/2 colorless artifact creature with no mechanic to enact, so the art's whole job is to depict what it IS: a built automaton whose hero signal is its name — "alloy," i.e. visibly mismatched metals fused into one body. Gen 5 lands that better than any other roll in the set. Its frame reads unmistakably as a heavy constructed automaton with a two-tone metal body — silver/iron limbs and shoulders set against a brass torso with a glowing amber furnace-core — so the "alloy" (different metals welded together) is legible at a glance rather than just a uniform gold golem. It has a clean, centered, imposing head-to-knee silhouette (strong at 64x32), riveted plate seams, and a rich but uncluttered soot-stained foundry behind it with a banked forge-glow that keeps the figure foregrounded. It is colorless-correct (all-metal palette, no WUBRG wash), and the foundry context defeats PixelLab's sprite-on-transparency pull. Gen 3 (its seed parent) and gen 1 were close runners-up, but gen 5's mixed-metal reading is the most card-true.
