Best enactment of the card's mechanic. Forest Forager pulls a Land out of the library and onto the battlefield, and this frame shows exactly that as an in-world action: a green-clad forager hunched mid-stride, hauling a large mossy slab of earth out of the deep wood. The silhouette is the clearest of the whole spread — instantly legible at 64x32 as "someone carrying a load of forest earth back" — and the hauled turf reads unmistakably as a chunk of land rather than just a backpack. Strong green color identity (moss, soil-brown, canopy light), good forest depth, and a committed, specific pose. The face reads more human than overtly elven, but the body language and the carried earth carry the Elf Druid forager identity completely. Chosen over the kneeling "raising land from the ground" coaxer variants (gen 02/03), which read slightly more amorphous, and over the other hauler rolls (05/06) whose loads drifted toward cloak/foliage rather than a clean earth-slab.
