Wind Drake is a vanilla 2/2 blue Dragon (a drake) named for the wind — a literal-card, not a story-card, so the art is a character note: a sleek, agile, airborne creature riding the air. This frame nails that. The cerulean-and-steel drake is caught mid-climb on a rising gust, wings raised high and tail whipping, which reads as active flight rather than a posed sprite — it enacts the airborne nature a drake should always show. The silhouette is the cleanest of the four rolls: the spread-winged flier is instantly legible at 64x32 against open pale-blue sky, with no muddying of the body into the background. Color identity is squarely blue (slate/cerulean scales) with a warm golden-cream belly accent and a crisp golden eye for focus. It edges out gen 01 (a calmer, equally clean banking glide) on dynamism while losing nothing in readability, and beats gen 02's stooping dive, whose darker body partially merged with the storm cloud and whose magenta accents drifted off the blue identity.
