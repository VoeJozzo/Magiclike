Day of Reckoning's only mechanic is "destroy ALL creatures" -- an indiscriminate white board wipe. This frame enacts that mechanic most truthfully: a field strewn with many distinct fallen bodies and crimson blood, all struck dead together beneath a single dominant shaft of holy silver-white divine radiance descending from a rift in black storm clouds. The whole field being dead IS the "all creatures" point, and it sidesteps the two misreads that weakened other rolls -- a single victim (would read as targeted removal) or armies still standing (reads pre-event). The cold white-gold light carries the card's white color identity cleanly, distinguishing a holy wrath from a black/necromantic one. Composition gives a strong readable silhouette at 64x32: a vertical light column over a horizontal band of corpses. Its foreground bodies register as distinct figures better than the muddier masses of gens 5/9, while keeping their crisp, dominant light.
