Rationale: Best balance of mechanic-enactment, color identity, and gestalt. A
white-robed figure is coalescing/stepping out of a radiant burst of white-gold
light in a dark cathedral -- which directly enacts Cloudshift's blink (exile
target creature, then immediately return it to the battlefield): the creature
reappears from a flash of light. Strong mono-white color identity (the card is
just W), the radiant cloud is the named light source, and the dark arched
interior gives real depth and contrast so it reads as a scene, not a sprite on
void. The figure stays clearly readable against the glow, and the symmetric
composition is striking at 64x32.

Close runners-up: gen 03 (figure half-dissolved into luminous cloud -- gorgeous
gestalt but skews gold/sepia, weak white identity, figure indistinct) and gen 08
(angel materializing in a white-gold burst -- dramatic but the body is partly
swallowed by the glow and it skews warm dawn).
