Rationale: Best mechanic-in-art enactment of the whole spread. Seal-Thief Courier's
trigger steals a card from the opponent's graveyard and lets YOU cast it, and this
frame literally shows the thief brandishing a stolen, glowing card between two fingers
toward the viewer, sly grin underlit by its light, a robbed grave behind him in a
black-blue night cemetery. The card-as-loot is unambiguous, the hooded human rogue
reads as a character (face + grin, not a blob), and the night-graveyard palette and
silhouette harmonize with the set's UB rogues (Royal Assassin, Shadowmage). The one
diagnosable flaw -- the stolen card glows magenta rather than the UB cerulean/violet --
is a cheap single-variable tweak (seed-locked color swap), exactly the kind of reachy,
nameable imperfection the skill prefers over a timid, generic frame.

Runner-up: gen_09 (seed 416536173) -- cleaner UB palette and a crisp "card torn free
from the grave" action, but reads slightly more necromancer than rogue (no visible
face/grin).
