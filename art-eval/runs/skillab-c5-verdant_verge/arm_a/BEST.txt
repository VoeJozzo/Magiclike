This frame best enacts the card's mechanic: Verdant Verge is an Artifact Land Forest that taps for green mana, so the art shows a constructed verdigris-bronze monolith — a crafted artifact — visibly radiating a bright green crystal core, i.e. a land that produces green mana made literal as a glowing source. The artifact-land duality is legible in the seam where oxidized machined metal meets living moss, ivy and a red toadstool at the base. At 64x32 it passes the silhouette test cleanly: one strong vertical standing-stone shape against a deep-green old-growth treeline, green color identity unambiguous. It was reached by seed-locking gen 1's composition (seed 414910775) and tweaking the prompt to brighten the crystal core, which held the framing while strengthening the hero element. Gen 5 (a tidy flanked-by-mushrooms variant) and gen 4 (a more ornate, moodier artifact-shrine) are the runners-up, but gen 3 has the best combination of saturated green identity, artifact-vs-nature seam, and clean readable silhouette.
