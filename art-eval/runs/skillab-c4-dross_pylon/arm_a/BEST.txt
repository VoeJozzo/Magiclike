This frame enacts both halves of the card's identity at once: it is a tall pylon built of rusted scrap rising from a wide heap of grey slag dross (the "dross" — the structure is visibly refuse-metal), and a bright vein of colorless mana light runs the full height of its core and pools/flows at the base (the mechanic — tap for {C}, a mana source you can see producing). The silhouette is the clearest in the set: a triangular tower-on-a-rubble-pile that will stay legible at 64x32. It beats gen 3 (clean vertical spire with a strong mana vein, but reads more like a generic rock spire than a heap of dross) and gen 4 (great dross pile, but the mana is a contained gem rather than a flowing channel). Gen 5 unifies the dross heap and the channeled mana flow, the two things the card is.
