This frame best enacts the card's signature mechanic: the demon extends an outstretched clawed hand offering a glowing molten-gold contract seal — the literal "bargain" the card is named for, the deal-marker it places on permanents. It is the clearest mechanic-in-art read of the set: an onlooker grasps "this demon is making you an offer" instantly. It satisfies Flying with a bold, readable winged silhouette (the strong literal-depiction exception the skill calls out), and it has the cleanest black-card color identity of the batch — a near-black hellish chamber with the gold/ember seal as the single warm accent, matching the consume_spirit / demonic_tutor anchors (deep dark + one warm light source). It is a seed-locked tweak of gen_03 that held the composition tightly while sharpening the held seal into a brighter, disc-like runic brand and adding menace to the demon's form, so it strictly improves on its already-strong parent.
