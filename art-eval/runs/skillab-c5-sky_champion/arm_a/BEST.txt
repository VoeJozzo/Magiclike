Sky Champion's defining mechanic is not merely Flying -- it is a flyer whose
arrival GRANTS flying to one of your creatures (its ETB trigger). The art must
enact that, not just decorate "a winged warrior." Gen 09 is the only frame where
BOTH figures are unmistakably airborne: a large white-gold winged spirit warrior
on the right, and a second bronze-armored soldier on the left lifted into the sky
with his own wing of light spread -- now flying alongside the champion. That is
the card happening: the Sky Champion has bestowed flight on an ally and they take
to the air together. It also honors the strong flying-literal convention for the
champion itself, carries clean white/gold color identity for a {W} card with a
distinct bronze ally for contrast, and reads instantly at 64x32 as "two warriors
flying together." Its visible flaw -- the ally's single prominent wing rather than
a symmetric pair -- is the kind of reaching, fixable imperfection the discipline
prefers over the more polished but mechanically timid gen 07 (a single radiant
angel above a still-grounded soldier), which depicts a blessing but not the act of
granting flight.
