Rationale: Best mechanic-in-art of the batch. Grave Digger's trigger returns a creature card from the graveyard to hand; this frame enacts that literally — a hulking green decaying zombie is hunched over the mouth of an open, stone-rimmed grave, hauling a recognizable PALE human body up out of the dark pit by the shoulders. The pale-flesh-vs-green-zombie contrast (a recurring trap in this set) finally reads clearly here, so the corpse registers as a separate "dead creature being raised," not as more zombie. Grave-mouth + cross + crescent moon give black/undead set coherence with the anchor arts (morticians_assistant, grave_curate, reaper_shade: muted, dark, green-tinged). Strong 64x32 silhouette: the bent zombie + pit + pale lifted form is one readable shape. Runners-up: gen 7 (full dig narrative with red shovel + half-buried pale corpse, but body reads as a heap) and gen 10 (great mood, weaker mechanic clarity). Down-ranked gen 1/8 as menacing-but-generic creature portraits that don't enact the recursion.
