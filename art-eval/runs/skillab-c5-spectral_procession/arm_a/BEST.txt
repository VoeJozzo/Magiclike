Spectral Procession's entire rules text is "create three 1/1 white Spirit tokens," so the art's one job is to make the eye count three spirits — and to feel like a procession, not a lineup. Gen 07 nails both. Three luminous cyan-white hooded spirits are caught mid-stride, leaning forward, clearly moving together in a single file: a procession enacted as motion rather than merely depicted as a row (this is what edges it past gen 08, which reads more as three figures standing). Each spirit carries a small warm candle-flame, a funeral-march narrative beat whose amber tone contrasts deliberately against the cold spectral cyan — color discipline on a white card with spectral creatures. The translucent glowing forms sit squarely in the established set vocabulary for spirits (echo_spirit / vengeful_spirit), and the dark churchyard with lamp-post markers grounds it as a real scene so the glow reads and it avoids the sprite-on-transparency trap. Best mechanic-enactment, best sense of "procession," strongest atmosphere.
