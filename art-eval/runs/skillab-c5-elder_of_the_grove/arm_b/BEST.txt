This is the only frame that enacts BOTH of the card's mechanics legibly at 64x32. The antlered, grey-bearded elven elder stands centered and symmetric, arms spread wide in a presiding pose framed by a natural archway of moss-slicked grove trunks - that authority reads as "elder/lord," the trait behind the +1/+1 buff to other Druids. And a bright verdant-green mana orb glows above EACH outstretched palm (two orbs total), directly enacting the "tap for GG" ability and matching the exact mana count. Full green color identity, a true scene rather than a sprite on negative space, and a strong central silhouette with two bright points. It was reached via seed-locked iteration from gen 1 (added channeled mana) through gen 3 (one orb) to gen 4 (two-orb symmetric presiding pose), each step fixing a named flaw rather than blind-rerolling. Gen 5 was the strong runner-up - the only frame to show the buff via flanking empowered druids - but it lacks the visible mana ability and has lower contrast.
