Best enacts the card's mechanic: Flame Summoner's ETB trigger deals 2 damage to a creature or player, and this frame shows exactly that happening — a tribal shaman in a wide conjuring stance on the left, a roaring arc of summoned orange flame crossing the frame, and a victim on the right staggering backward with arm raised, tunic alight. The attacker-to-victim asymmetry is the whole point and it reads cleanly at 64x32 (unlike gen 03/04 which drift toward a symmetric duel). Correct red color identity (warm orange fire as the sole light source against a dark scorched night), scene-embedded rather than a sprite floating on empty space, and the "summoner" read is honored by fire being conjured/directed rather than a thrown object. The figures are dark silhouettes — a fixable detail, not a failure of the gestalt; the frame reaches for a specific mechanic-true scene and lands it. Anchors well against char (battlemage burning a victim by firelight).
