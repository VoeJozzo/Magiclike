This frame enacts Flame Summoner's mechanic better than any other roll: its only ability is an enter-the-battlefield trigger that deals 2 damage to a creature or player, so the art shows the summoning AS the attack. The canvas reads, left to right, as caster -> conjured fireball -> impact on a victim: a dark tribal-shaman silhouette on the left with arm thrust forward, a bright round fireball burst, and a clearly figural bandit on the right being engulfed and recoiling. The fire is the sole light source (Char's proven move), giving pure red color-identity and a high-contrast, legible two-figure silhouette at 64x32. It was reached by diagnose-then-iterate: gen 4 established the discrete-projectile composition but the burst landed in the empty gap short of the target; a single seed-locked tweak (gen 5) moved the impact directly onto the victim's chest without losing the frame. Later tweaks (gen 7 brighten-caster) and fresh rerolls (gen 6, gen 8) either failed to improve the named flaw or dropped the victim entirely, leaving a beautiful but mechanic-mute "shaman casting fire" sprite. Gen 5 keeps both the clean silhouette and the unambiguous "arrival burns a victim" beat.
