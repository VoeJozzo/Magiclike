Gen 03 best enacts Pyromaniac's mechanic: a 1/1 fire shaman whose arrival flings 1 damage at any target. The frame shows exactly that moment — a gaunt shaman in scorched crimson robes, hunched and lunging in clean side profile, arm extended, with a discrete bead of orange flame caught mid-flight streaking across the gap toward a distinct victim figure at the right who recoils as it reaches him. It reads as a *thrown point* of fire (a 1-damage ping), not a conflagration, which is the precise mechanic. Two clearly separated, readable figures at 64x32; strong red color identity in the robes and flame; warm light sourced from the spark itself. It came from a seed-locked tweak of gen 02 — the lock held the strong single-shaman composition while the edit added the projectile and victim, giving the cleanest mechanic-in-art of the set. Gen 04 was a strong rival but its fire engulfs the scene (reads as a blaze, not a 1-damage spark) and half-swallows the victim; gen 05 pulled the flame back into the hand and lost the in-flight clarity.
