Day of Reckoning is a white sorcery that destroys ALL creatures (a board wipe), and its name is literally a day of judgment. Gen 8 enacts both at once: enormous glowing white-gold scales of divine judgment dominate the frame, descending beams of holy light pouring down onto a full-width row of small dark silhouetted combatants at the base who are being washed away. The scales-of-judgment are the single most on-name, specific, nameable symbol for this card -- far stronger than a generic light pillar (gen 1) or a fiery eruption (gen 4/6) -- and the row of doomed figures across the entire bottom edge supplies the "all creatures" breadth that a single-victim image would miss. It has the cleanest read of the spread: the balance is crisp and unmistakable at 64x32 (strong central silhouette), the white/gold radiant palette is exactly right for a white card, and it harmonizes with the set's backlit white-row compositions (rally_the_troops) while contrasting smite_the_wicked's chaotic fire with cold divine order. It is the seed-locked tighten of gen 7 and reads noticeably cleaner (more symmetric, less busy crossbar) than its parent.
