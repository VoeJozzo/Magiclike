This frame is the strongest enactment of the card's mechanic: Sudden Vines targets a Land and turns it into a 1/1 Creature, so the art shows a grey stone boulder-body unmistakably reading as a piece of animated *land*, brought to life by lush bright-emerald vines that erupt from the broken soil and wrap up its side. The vines are the dominant visual element, which is correct for a mono-green (G) vine spell, and the single glowing green eye plus the active, lunging crouch sell the "sudden" flash-speed moment of the ground heaving up into a creature. Compared to the cleaner-but-more-passive rock-creature rolls (gen 3, gen 5), this one keeps the land-becomes-creature read while adding dynamism and far stronger green color identity; its silhouette at 64x32 stays legible as a single crouched stone creature.
