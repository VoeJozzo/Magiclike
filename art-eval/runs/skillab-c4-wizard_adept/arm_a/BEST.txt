Wizard Adept's mechanic is "whenever you cast another spell, draw a card then discard one" — a studious wizard who pulls a fresh page of knowledge from his book every time magic is cast nearby. Gen 06 enacts that most clearly of the set: a hooded blue adept facing the viewer at his lectern, raising a single crisp page that glows bright blue with runes — unmistakably the "draw," the fresh page coming into hand — over an open spellbook, the source he's drawing from. It has the strongest, cleanest reading at 64x32 (centered subject, a clear two-shape silhouette of figure + glowing page), the best blue color identity for a U creature, and the most polished, card-frame-ready composition with symmetric bookshelf framing and a central column of blue light. The discard/falling-page half never fully rendered across the seed-lock tweaks, but the half that did land — drawing the glowing page — is the more legible and iconic half of the mechanic, and this frame nails it with more atmosphere and finish than the busier alternatives (gen 04/05) or the discard-attempt rerolls.
