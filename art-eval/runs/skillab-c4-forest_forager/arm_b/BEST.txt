This frame is the cleanest enactment of Forest Forager's mechanic — its ETB pulls a Land out of the library and onto the battlefield. The druid is shown mid-act, kneeling and hauling a distinct clod of living earth up out of the forest floor, the lifted chunk crowned with moss, dangling roots, and a small sapling while soil crumbles away. That reads unmistakably as "foraging a piece of land into the world," not as the generic subject-prior trap of "an elf standing in a forest." The silhouette is strong at 64x32 (hunched figure + raised mass of earth), the color identity is solidly green, and the warm dawn light slanting through the emerald canopy onto the freshly torn ground gives it depth and a real scene rather than a sprite on negative space. Gen 04 was a close runner-up (adds a magical glow on the sapling) but its land reads more as a mound being tended than one being lifted; gen 01 wins on mechanic legibility.
