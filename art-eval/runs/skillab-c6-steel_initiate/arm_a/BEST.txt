Steel Initiate is a 1W 2/2 Human Soldier whose attack trigger gains its controller 1 life, so the art needed to enact "charging forward produces life/energy" on a young, plain-STEEL (not gilded) recruit — a deliberate set-coherence contrast against the gold-plated veterans (holy_zealot, field_marshal). This frame is the strongest fusion: a steel-plate soldier in a deep, committed crouched lunge with the sword thrust forward and a glowing white-gold radiant blade, red banner at left and red cape at right giving the white card its warm accent colors, set against a golden-hour battlefield with a soft white light-bloom behind his shoulder. The gestalt at full size reads instantly as "armored soldier charging, wreathed in radiant energy" — the attack and the life-giving glow are the same motion, which is exactly the mechanic. It also wins on the silhouette test (one clean, dynamic lunging shape at 64x32), correct white-color identity (silver steel + white glow, no gold trim), and the most dramatic, ambitious composition of the run. Its closest rival, gen 09, has a slightly cleaner literal "aura centered on the chest" read, but gen 08 is the more striking and complete image overall while still enacting the lifegain.
