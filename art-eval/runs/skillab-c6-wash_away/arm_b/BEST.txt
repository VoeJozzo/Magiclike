Nominee rationale:
Wash Away is a UC2 blue Sorcery that bounces ALL creatures (a board-wide
"return everything to hand"). The art that best ENACTS that mechanic is the
one that shows multiple, distinct creatures actually being carried off by a
flood -- not a pretty wave on its own.

Gen 5 (aerial-diagonal courtyard flood) delivers exactly that: three clearly
different creatures -- a red-helmed soldier, a green beast/goblin, and a
blue-cloaked figure -- caught in a glowing cyan surge sweeping across stone
flagstones. "All creatures washed away" is read directly off the frame. It is
also the strongest set-coherence match: deep cerulean/sapphire palette,
painterly pixel clusters, and real environmental depth, the same blue-card
vocabulary as the studious_research anchor.

Close runner-up: gen_01 (seed 2050819176) -- a towering curling wave with dark
tumbling bodies in its foaming base. It has the best pure 64x32 silhouette
(instantly legible as the card's title action) and is the pick if maximum
at-a-glance readability is preferred over showing distinct multiple victims.

Discarded for weak mechanic-enactment: gen_08 and gen_02 (beautiful waves but
the victims didn't render -- subject-prior won); gen_06 (caster + wave, but no
creatures shown being swept); gen_09 (bright daylight drifted to a peaceful
landscape, off blue-card identity); gen_03/gen_07 (atmospheric but figures read
as standing/riding, not being washed away); gen_04 (good single-victim close-up
but only one creature, less "all").
