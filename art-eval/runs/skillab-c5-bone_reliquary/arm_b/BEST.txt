This frame enacts all three of the card's type-line identities at once and shows the mechanic happening. Bone Reliquary is an Artifact Land Swamp that taps for black mana, so the art needs to read simultaneously as a crafted relic, a bone-filled place, and a black-mana source. Gen 7 delivers: an ornate tarnished-gold reliquary casket (Artifact) overflowing with bone-white skulls, with more skulls strewn on the mossy swamp banks to either side (a reliquary OF bones, not a generic chest), and violet-black death mana visibly welling out of the casket and dripping down into the still black swamp water (the black-mana source, depicted as an active spill rather than a static aura). The casket gives a strong boxy silhouette, the gestalt holds at 64x32, and the side skulls plus the dripping-mana reflections give it scene depth that the cleaner contenders (gen 3, gen 5) lacked. It also matches the set's swamp palette anchored by the basic swamp art (deep teal-black wetland, warm reflected glow). Chosen over gen 5 (also strong, mana spilling) because gen 7's flanking skulls and reflected rivulets read more clearly as a bone-strewn LAND rather than a single object, and the mana-as-mana-source enactment is the most legible in the run.
