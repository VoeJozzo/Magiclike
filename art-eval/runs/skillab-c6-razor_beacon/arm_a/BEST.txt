This frame enacts the card's identity most cleanly. Razor Beacon is a colorless Artifact Creature - Construct, 2/2, with flying, and the name fuses two ideas: "razor" (sharp bladed metal) and "beacon" (a glowing signal-light). Gen 5 shows exactly that - a flying construct built from broad razor-edged steel blades fanned in a symmetric metallic starburst, with a single dominant amber beacon-lens burning as a bright eye-core at its center. The flying keyword is satisfied via the strongest cross-game convention (the construct is airborne, banking over a dawn cloud sea), the "razor" reads in the radiating blade-spokes, and the "beacon" is the unmistakable glowing focal core. Cold steel + dark gunmetal seams keep it colorless-artifact in identity, with the one warm accent (the beacon glow) justified by the name. It was reached via seed-lock iteration: gen 1 established the starburst-with-glowing-core composition, gen 3 broadened and symmetrized the blades and made the core dominant, and gen 5 (this frame) resolved gen 3's one ambiguous lower stub into a deliberate tapering steel keel - giving the cleanest, most legible silhouette of the five, with elegant feather-blades and a crisp radiant heart.
