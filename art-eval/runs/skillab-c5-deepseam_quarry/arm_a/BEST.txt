This frame best enacts Deepseam Quarry's defining mechanic: sacrificing the quarry to drag the greatest buried creature out of the earth and reanimate it as yours. It reads cleanly at 64x32 as a colossal fossil-stone creature standing deep inside a mine shaft, framed by terraced rock walls, with a bright amber ore vein -- the literal "deepseam" -- cracking down the right wall and a single glowing amber eye. The colossus material reads as exhumed dead stone-and-bone rather than a generic mech (which crept into the brighter-eye tweak, gen 05) or an indistinct cave-blob (gen 06), and the silhouette is the most legible hero shape of the set. It harmonizes with forest_titan's hulking-earth-creature framing while taking the golem_forge underlight palette. The one fixable miss -- it stands on the floor rather than visibly heaving up out of broken earth -- is exactly the diagnosable kind of flaw the skill says to prefer over a timid, generic frame; the gestalt (huge dead thing drawn from the glowing seam) lands.
