This frame best enacts the card's mechanic. Wind Dancer is a 1/1 blue Faerie with Flying and a name that promises she rides the wind, so the art must show her airborne AND show the wind as a visible mechanism. Gen 04 delivers both: the faerie is clearly aloft mid-twirl with translucent gossamer wings, and the moving air is rendered as legible curving wind-current streaks arcing across the lower frame, with cherry-blossom petals streaming off a framing branch into that current and a soft cloud at the right edge. It reads unmistakably as "dancing on the wind," not merely a static winged sprite. The silhouette holds at 64x32 (a single arched winged dancer), the twilight-blue sky carries the color identity, and the composition is the most complete scene of the batch. Gen 03 was the close runner-up with a stronger blue palette and a cleaner viewer-facing face, but its wind motion is subtler; Gen 04 wins on mechanic-enactment, which is the deciding lens.
