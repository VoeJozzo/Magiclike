This is the strongest of the eight rolls on both axes that matter. As a figure it reads instantly as a colossal artifact construct: a massive, symmetric, riveted body in weathered verdigris bronze and iron, with a hot glowing amber eye-core dead center — clean material identity, imposing silhouette, no genre-default sloppiness. As mechanic-in-art it enacts trample where every "tiny soldier underfoot" attempt failed (the model's subject-prior kept rendering "big construct standing" and dropping the victim): here trample is shown as an unstoppable mass planted on a shattering stone wall, with broken masonry and pale dust bursting outward at its feet and distant broken battlements on the horizon. It walks through stone — nothing stops it. It also harmonizes with the set's existing artifact/large-creature vocabulary (dominant figure filling the frame over a real environment, per the alloy_construct and forest_titan anchors). Gen 1/3 had a better figure but illegible victims; Gen 4/7 were close but with hazier rubble; Gen 5 has the cleanest marriage of legible colossus + readable trample.
