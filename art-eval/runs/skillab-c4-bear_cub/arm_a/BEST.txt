Bear Cub is a vanilla 1/1 green creature with no keywords or effects, so it is a literal-card: the art's only job is to read unmistakably as a small, young, harmless bear cub, in green-forest color identity. Gen 4 nails that better than any other roll. The cub is plump, round, seated on its rump, and clearly a baby; it is reaching up to bat at a clearly-visible bumblebee, a curiosity/play beat that reinforces "young and harmless" and gives the frame a real little scene rather than a sprite on void. The fur is a true warm brown (gens 2 and 3 drifted fox-orange), the lighting is dappled and layered with genuine forest depth (path, ferns, canopy), and the silhouette reads cleanly as a small quadruped cub at 64x32. It harmonizes with the saturated, scene-set register of the green-creature anchors (feral_stalker, wolfbriar_elemental) while staying soft and youthful as the card demands.
