Gray Ogre is a vanilla red 2/2 with no mechanic to enact, so this is a literal card: the art's job is a strong, immediately-readable ogre, not a story beat. Gen 1 is the cleanest full-body read in the spread, which is what a plain creature's body type (2/2 grunt) wants. It carries the card's one distinctive signal -- coarse gray hide -- clearly setting it apart from the set's red-skinned sibling vexing_ogre. Color identity is solid: an off-frame campfire plus the cave-mouth fire arc rim-light the gray shoulders in warm orange, placing it squarely in the set's red-brute vocabulary (vexing_ogre, furnace_brute). The hunched, tusked, roaring pose gives brutish menace with a clean centered silhouette that reads at 64x32. Gen 4 (worm's-eye raised-club swing) and Gen 6 (snarling close-up portrait) are the strongest alternates and reach hard, but Gen 1 is the most complete, on-vocabulary, legible creature frame. A seed-locked tweak (Gen 7) confirmed this composition is the keeper of the direction -- the club-overhead edit fought the seed's prior, so the original holds.
