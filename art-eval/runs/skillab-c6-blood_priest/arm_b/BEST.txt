This frame best enacts Blood Priest's mechanic (its arrival drains the opponent's life). A hooded black-robed cleric stands at a stone blood-altar, hands raised mid-incantation, as a column of glowing crimson blood-essence rises from a bronze chalice into his palms -- the literal "life being taken and channeled" the card's gain_life:-2-to-opponent trigger represents. It reads cleanly as a whole: strong centered silhouette, crisp hero elements (raised hands, glowing chalice, vertical blood-column), proper black/crimson color identity for a B-cost cleric, and an atmospheric soot-stained cathedral background (a scene, not a sprite on void). It harmonizes with the set's dark-cleric anchors (cult_priest, soul_reaper). Close runners-up: gen 07 (same composition pushed to full crimson saturation -- more dramatic but slightly muddier hands and over-glowed background) and gen 06 (the most menacing blood-smeared portrait, best face/color identity, but it implies the drain rather than showing the channeling rite). Gen 02 wins on clarity of mechanic + gestalt.
