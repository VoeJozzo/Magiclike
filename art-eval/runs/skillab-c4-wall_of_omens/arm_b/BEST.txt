This frame best enacts Wall of Omens' mechanic. The card is a 0/4 Wall (a pure defensive structure) that draws a card when it enters — an omen revealed. The art fuses both: a weathered pale-limestone defensive rampart spanning the full width of the frame, with a gold-filigree-framed circular oculus carved into its center showing a ghostly blue-white prophetic vision, like a scrying window. The silhouette reads instantly at 64x32 as "a wall with a glowing eye of prophecy," the color identity is correctly white (pale stone, gold, sacred blue-white glow), and the glow is the sole light source so the omen is the hero of the scene. It edges out the cleaner gen_04 (which reads as a pure radiant portal/orb, less of a legible *omen*, and splits the wall into two halves) and the blank-glow gen_01 (whose oculus showed no vision). The seed-locked tweak from gen_01 held the composition while making the vision inside read as a faint foretold scene — exactly the portent the card promises.
