This frame is the cleanest synthesis of the card's two demands: it reads as a CAPTAIN (a clearly dominant foreground hero in detailed silver plate with a dramatic crimson cape, raising a longsword) AND it enacts the mechanic (whenever you cast another spell, pump ALL your creatures +1/+0) — his raised blade blazes with tall white-blue soulfire, and the rank of allied knights behind/beside him have their own raised swords lit with the SAME white soulfire, so the buff is visibly propagating across the whole unit. It also nails the white color identity (pure white-blue light, not the orange that crept into gen 1) where the soulfire is literally the only light source. Compared to gen 3 (great hero read but only one clearly-lit ally) and gen 4 (purest "all blades lit" but a flat row of near-identical figures with no captain hierarchy), gen 5 gets both the heroic captain silhouette and the multi-blade team-empowerment in one strong, atmospheric composition.
