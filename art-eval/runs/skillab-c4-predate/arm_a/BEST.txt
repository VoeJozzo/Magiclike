This frame enacts Predate's mechanic — pump your creature, then fight an opponent's — as a single readable moment. A glowing emerald-green wolf-beast (the +1/+1-empowered predator, its musculature lit green) has lunged across and sunk its open red jaws into the throat of a pale golden-tan stag, with a crimson blood smear bridging the bite. The two creatures read as distinctly different things (the skill's fix for the multi-subject merge: explicit headcount plus a hard green-vs-gold color contrast), are the same scale, and both are fully visible head to tail with clean silhouettes at 64x32. The predator is unambiguously the aggressor winning the fight — the prey is rearing and bleeding — which mirrors the card's resolution (your buffed creature overpowering theirs). It also keeps a real scene behind the action (dim autumn forest, bracken, mist) so it reads as a depicted fight rather than a sprite on negative space. Chosen over the cleaner-but-pre-contact gen 5 because gen 7 keeps that frame's legibility while adding the actual bite-contact and blood that make the "fight" land.
