Spitfire Bastion is a Wall (1/3, no attack) whose only action is to tap and spit 1 damage at any target — so the art has to show two things at once: that it is a fortification, and that it actively spits fire. Gen 7 nails both. A carved stone gargoyle/serpent head is unmistakably built into a wide masonry rampart (clean brick coursing and crenellation-edge highlights fill the left half), and it spits a sharp, directional jet of orange-yellow-red flame across the right half with a long trailing ember plume. The fire-bolt is the literal 1-damage ping made visible, issuing from a mouth in the wall — the mechanic is enacted, not just decorated. The wide left-structure / right-flame composition is an ideal silhouette for the 64x32 landscape frame, the red/orange flame carries the card's red color identity, and the stone reads brighter and crisper here than in its sibling rolls (gen 3), making it the most legible at size.
