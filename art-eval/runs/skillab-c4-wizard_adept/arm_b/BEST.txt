This roll most clearly enacts Wizard Adept's mechanic: a hooded blue-robed scholar seated at a book-strewn study desk, a bright cerulean glowing scroll spiraling up into his raised hand — the "cast another spell, draw a card" beat made literal as knowledge flowing in — while his other hand reaches toward a stack of pages (the discard). It carries the card's blue identity cleanly (cerulean robe, blue arcane glow as the sole light source), reads as a frail 1/3 thinker rather than a fighter, and is set-coherent with the blue-wizard/scholar anchors. The silhouette is strong and legible at 64x32: a centered seated figure with an unmistakable in-flowing glow arc. It edged out gen 1 (same composition, slightly muddier face) and gen 3's only nameable flaw — the discarded page merges into the side stack rather than tumbling distinctly — is a minor, fixable detail in a frame that is genuinely reaching for the mechanic rather than settling for a generic casting wizard (the failure mode gens 2 and 4 fell into).
