This frame is the cleanest enactment of the card's mechanic: a quarry/mine that hauls a dead giant up out of the deepest seam and reanimates it under your control. The canvas alone, with no knowledge of intent, reads as a colossal horned beast skeleton inside a dark stone cavern, flanked by vertical rusted chains/mine rigging, with the exposed mineral seam glowing cold blue at the base. The decisive detail is the single bright blue glowing eye in the crisply-rendered skull — it instantly signals necromantic reanimation and gives the 64x32 a strong focal point and silhouette. Color identity fits a colorless earth/mine card (slate greys, bone, no off-theme red), and the cold blue glow carries the "raising the dead" beat without drifting into a colored faction. It beat its siblings on skull legibility and on the prominence of the reanimation glow (gen 1 had no excavation read, gen 4 dissolved into a spectral blur, gen 6's blue glow was too small).
