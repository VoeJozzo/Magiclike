This frame best enacts Sky Champion's mechanic: a winged spirit-warrior on the right with large, bright white-and-gold feathered wings reaches out and clasps the arm of a grounded comrade on the left, hauling him toward flight. The card's defining rule is "enters the battlefield and grants flying to another of your creatures," and the canvas alone -- with zero knowledge of intent -- shows exactly that asymmetric act of one airborne champion bestowing flight on a grounded ally via a connecting arm-clasp. It has the most legible, brightest white-gold feathered wing of the whole batch (strongest white/colorless color-identity match), a clean two-figure silhouette readable at 64x32, and a clear dawn-sky-over-meadow scene rather than a sprite on negative space. Gen 7 told a more complete "grant in progress" story (ally sprouting his own wings) but its bodies darkened away from white identity and its both-winged read blurred who grants to whom; gen 6's cleaner, whiter, asymmetric composition wins on legibility and color discipline.
