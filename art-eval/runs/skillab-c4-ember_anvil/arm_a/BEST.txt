Ember Anvil is an Artifact Land Mountain that taps for red mana, so the art must read as a thing that is both an anvil and a source of forge-fire/red mana. This roll nails it: a crisp, unmistakable horned-anvil silhouette sits dead-center on a stone block, the iron glowing red-hot, with roaring molten forge-fire erupting around and beneath it and a shower of embers rising — the object itself is visibly the heat/mana engine, enacting the mechanic rather than just decorating the theme. Among the fiery-forge variants (gens 4 and 5) it has the cleanest anvil silhouette while keeping the dramatic ember-storm, and it carries the red color identity emphatically. It beats the calmer cavern reads (gens 2 and 3) on energy and on "this is a power source," and beats gen 1 (whose fire pooled in a top-bowl, reading like a brazier) on correctly placing the anvil as the hero shape.
