BEST: grizzly_bears_gen_04_seed1484728453.png

Rationale:
Grizzly Bears is the canonical literal vanilla 2/2 -- no mechanic to enact, so
the art just has to be an unmistakable grizzly with a strong silhouette and clear
green-forest color identity. Gen 4 delivers the most presence of the spread: a
big brown bear charging head-on toward the viewer at ground level, jaws open,
amber eyes catching the light, forepaws planted on a forest trail with real depth
behind it in close pine trunks and undergrowth. It reads instantly as "a bear is
coming at you" even at 64x32 -- a fitting read for an attacking creature -- and it
reaches for an actual moment (the charge) rather than a static stock pose, in line
with the skill's reach-don't-hedge principle. Its only flaw is the slightly chunky
forepaws/chest, which is the fixable kind, not timidity.

Close runners-up:

- gen 02 (seed 906360648): textbook crisp side profile lumbering through birches;
  most "stock card art" legible.
- gen 08 (seed 1145271703): atmospheric head-and-shoulders emerging from ferns,
  strong menacing face; good ambush mood.
