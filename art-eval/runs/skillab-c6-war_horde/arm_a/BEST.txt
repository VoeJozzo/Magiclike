This frame best enacts both words of the card's name. War Horde is a vanilla 4/3 red Goblin Warrior with no abilities, so it is a literal-card: the art must show a horde of goblins at war, not a story. Gen 4 delivers exactly that gestalt — a burly green-skinned goblin warlord dead center, a single large notched iron cleaver raised high and legible above his head, leading a dense pack of smaller goblins that flank him on both sides with torches and weapons, all surging out of a burning crimson-orange sky over a churned battlefield. "War" reads in the firelit charge and raised blade; "horde" reads in the countable flanking mass. It carries strong red color identity (crimson dusk + torch glow), has the cleanest readable silhouette of the spread (central figure + symmetric flanking crowd), and harmonizes with the existing goblin set's palette (yellow-green skin, rusted iron, dark leather seen in goblin_rabble) while differentiating through motion and a hero-leads-the-charge composition. It was a seed-locked refinement of gen 3 that fixed gen 3's two flaws — an ambiguous/blobby raised weapon and a distracting dark bush in the corner — both resolved here.
