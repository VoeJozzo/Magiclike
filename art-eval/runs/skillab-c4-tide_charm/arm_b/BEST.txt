Of the seven rolls this one best enacts Tide Charm's modal nature as a single, legible casting gesture. A blue-caped mage in clean side profile thrusts an arm forward, projecting a glowing cerulean orb (a glyph/card shape visible inside it = Foresee) that arcs directly into a curling crescent wave of seawater, and a small bright-green frog-creature is clearly being swept in that wave at the mage's feet (= Bounce, return-a-creature). The orb-to-wave is one continuous flow of magic, so the art reads as the charm *doing* its modes rather than decorating the water theme. Color discipline is tightest here: an all-cerulean/sapphire palette with the green creature as a deliberate single point of contrast so the bounce target reads as a distinct thing. Silhouette holds at 64x32 (caped figure + extended arm + wave arc). Gen 7 (its seed-lock child) is prettier in places but splits the orb and wave into separate decorative elements and drifts toward a generic hatted-wizard read; gen 6 keeps them connected and active, which is the whole point.
