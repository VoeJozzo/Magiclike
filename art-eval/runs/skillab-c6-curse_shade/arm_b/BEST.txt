Rationale: This is the only frame that enacts the card's mechanic AS a scene with both
actors legible. Curse Shade is a 2/2 Specter whose death-trigger exiles a creature — the
shade reaches out and claims a victim. Gen 1 shows a tattered wine-purple specter (its right
edge fraying into smoke, reading simultaneously as incorporeal shade-form and as itself dying)
with one bony arm outstretched, gripping an armored silver knight by the arm. The specter's
hollow eye glows cold violet — the only light source — and the silver knight contrasts cleanly
against the purple shade so it reads as a separate victim, not "more shade." Clean two-figure
silhouette at 64x32, fully on black/purple color identity, and it harmonizes with the set's
existing shade vocabulary (reaper_shade) while adding the curse-reach the card is about.

Runners-up: gen_02 (green monster dragged into a void rift — strong exile/erasure read and
great color contrast, but the shade itself dissolved into the void and is no longer legible as
a character) and gen_06 (a textbook specter portrait matching reaper_shade, but no victim and
no mechanic). gen_03/05 collapsed to a single generic armored figure with the shade and the
ash-erasure failing to render.
