Prey Upon's only effect is "fight" between your creature and an opponent's, and the name frames it as a predator preying on prey. This frame enacts that mechanic the most directly of the set: a coarse gray timber wolf is hunched bodily on top of a sprawled tawny deer, head buried at the deer's neck, mauling it — the predator is literally upon the prey, not merely near it or standing over a finished kill. Both animals are large, same-scale, and unmistakably legible at 64x32 (the deer's antler rack reads cleanly to the right), giving the required two-subject read for a fight spell. It carries full green color identity — a sunlit old-growth forest scene, no floating-sprite emptiness — and harmonizes with the set's predator-in-grass anchor (feral_stalker). It out-reaches the cleaner "standing over the kill" frames (gen 3, gen 5, gen 7) by showing the violent act in progress; its only flaw is a slight tangle where the wolf's far legs overlap the deer's body, which is exactly the fixable, diagnosable kind of flaw the skill says to prefer over a timid, generic alternative.
