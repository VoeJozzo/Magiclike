Rationale:
Best mechanic-in-art of the batch. Devastation Tide bounces ALL opponent creatures
(returns them to hand), and this frame enacts exactly that: a cluster of distinct,
countable armored soldiers stand on a rocky outcrop in the foreground while a towering
turquoise tsunami rears up and bears down on them from the left/behind -- the tide is
visibly about to sweep these creatures off the field. Reads instantly at a glance.

Why it beats the close finalists:
- Gen 8 (seed 638291456): the most beautiful wave of all, but the swept figures are
  small specks in the foam -- the "bounce all creatures" mechanic reads thin.
- Gen 5 (seed 913596967): strong on both axes (commanding crest + ~4 figures in the
  surge), the runner-up; but figures read as running rather than being overtaken, and
  Gen 9's victims are more distinct/countable.
- Gen 7 (seed 1937829354): gorgeous wave but the foreground figure reads as a lone
  warrior RIDING the wave on a raft -- inverts the mechanic (survivor, not swept-away).

Gen 9 delivers the strongest silhouette test ("a great wave rearing over a group of
doomed figures"), the clearest multi-creature legibility, tight blue/teal color
identity matching the set's blue-spell vocabulary (anchor: Tide Charm), and rocky
foreground contrast that keeps the cool palette from going flat.
