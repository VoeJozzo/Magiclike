This frame best enacts Sicken's mechanic — a black -2/-2 curse on an opponent's creature. A hooded plague-witch in tattered black robes (the caster, black mana identity) reaches out and channels a sickly emerald-and-black miasma onto a once-brawny warrior on the right, who recoils in a pained crouch, his body visibly wasting: gaunt, ribby, hunched, skin gone reddish-grey and clinging to bone. The clear cause-and-effect line from the witch's hand to the withering victim, with the green disease-glow filling the space between, makes the debuff legible as an action rather than decoration — you see strength being drained out of the creature (the -2/-2). Two distinct, well-silhouetted figures, both fully visible, with correct black/sickly-green color identity. It was produced by seed-locking the strong gen_02 composition and tweaking only the victim's description toward visible wasting, which sharpened exactly that element while preserving the composition. It reads as the act of sickening, edging out gen_05's more static fully-emaciated aftermath.
