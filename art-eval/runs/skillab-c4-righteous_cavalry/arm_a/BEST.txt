Righteous Cavalry is a 4/3 white Human Knight with no special rules text — a literal card whose art should embody what it IS: aggressive holy cavalry. This frame enacts that directly. The horse-and-rider read as a single clean galloping wedge (the silhouette test passes cleanly at 64x32), and the couched lance is leveled forward as a crisp horizontal line — the single most important "charge" cue, and the element that sells the card's offense-leaning 4/3 body. White-and-gold heraldry and the streaming white banner carry the W color identity and the "Righteous" theme, while the sunlit battlefield plain with a distant troop line gives real scene depth (not a sprite floating on negative space) and a sense of an army on the move. Compared to the alternates: gen 02 traded the lance for a billowing cape (loses the charge note), gen 03 is a near-twin whose banner muddied toward brown, and gen 04 had the prettiest sky but dropped the lance entirely. Gen 01 is the one that both reads as a mounted knight charging AND keeps the lance, so it wins on mechanic-fit, legibility, and color identity together.
