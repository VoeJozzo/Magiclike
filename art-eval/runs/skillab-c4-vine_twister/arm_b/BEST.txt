Vine Twister is a 2/2 Treefolk whose only mechanic is granting trample to one of your creatures when it arrives, so the art should show a treefolk bestowing overrunning strength on an ally. Gen 2 is the only frame that enacts that: a clearly anthropomorphic bark-skinned treefolk stands center as the hero subject, haloed by an emerald energy aura, with a distinct smaller red creature on the lower-right as the recipient ally — the two-figure "grant to your creature" reading lands at 64x32, which is hard to hold at this size. It also nails green color identity and keeps an unmistakable treefolk silhouette (not a literal tree). Competing directions failed: the overrun-through-a-wall concept never rendered a readable shattering barrier across four seeds, and seed-locked tweaks toward a cleaner ally link (gens 3, 5, 7) drifted the treefolk into a plain giant tree with a tiny bystander, losing both the character and the mechanic. Gen 4 has the strongest standalone silhouette but reads as a static portrait rather than the card's action. Gen 2 best balances readable subject, color identity, and mechanic enactment.
