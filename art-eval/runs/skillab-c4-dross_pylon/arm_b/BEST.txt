This frame is the cleanest fusion of all four criteria. Silhouette: a single dark, rivet-banded industrial spire dominating the frame against a pale sky — unmistakably a pylon/tower, the strongest readable vertical shape of the run. Name fidelity: it reads as corroded slag-iron (dross) built into a tall pylon, true to both halves of the card's name. Mechanic-enactment: a bright column of colorless mana erupts from the crown with floating diamond motes — the tap-for-{C} made literally visible as the structure venting power. Color identity: the mana is pure silver-white and diamond-grey, no color drift (gen 1's plume had a pink tint; this seed-locked tweak fixed it while holding the same composition). It beat gen 5 (same concept, fresh seed) on plume vividness and light/dark contrast, and beat gen 2/4 on being clearly a fabricated artifact structure rather than a smoking volcano or a thin light-beam antenna.
