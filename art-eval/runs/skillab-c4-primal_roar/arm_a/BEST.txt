Primal Roar is a green mass-pump sorcery (+2/+2 to ALL your creatures), so the art has to show two things at once: the roar that triggers it and the whole team visibly empowered. Gen 3 does exactly that. The lead dire wolf is unmistakably mid-roar — jaws gaping, teeth bared, head up — and the pack of wolves on the right is bristling, swelling, and outlined in deep emerald primal energy with green-glowing eyes, so the buff is rendered as a visible effect spreading across multiple creatures, not just decoration. It enacts the mechanic (cause: the roar; effect: the whole pack powered up) rather than merely theming it. The silhouette reads cleanly at 64x32 (rearing roaring wolf, head thrown back, with a cluster of empowered packmates), the green energy threads the scene for solid color identity, and the dusk old-growth forest grounds it in the set's green-creature palette. Gen 1 had the best hero wolf but no visible buff; Gen 5 was cleaner but tame on the energy; Gen 4 was gorgeously green but collapsed the pack to a single companion. Gen 3 is the best union of mechanic-enactment, readable gestalt, and color identity.
