Wildfire Devil's entire identity is its death trigger: when it dies, it deals 3 damage to any target. So the art had to enact the death-as-detonation, not depict a calm demon. This frame nails it: a horned, winged demon centered and silhouetted black against a massive radial fireball that fills the frame edge to edge, with a searing molten core erupting from its chest and fire bursting outward in every direction. The creature is unmistakably the SOURCE of the explosion — the mechanic (its death is a 3-damage blast) is legible from the canvas alone. It wins over the close runner-up (gen 3) on two axes: the black-silhouette-against-blast composition reads more crisply at 64x32 (clean demon shape, unmistakable explosion), and the color identity is pure red/orange across the whole frame with no cooler-toned intrusion. Strong silhouette, strong color identity, scene-not-sprite, and a clear gestalt at size.
