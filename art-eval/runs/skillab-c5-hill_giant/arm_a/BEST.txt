Hill Giant is a vanilla 3/3 red Creature - Giant with no keywords, triggers, or effects, so it is a literal-card, not a story-card: the art's job is a big, threatening, instantly-readable beater, not a clever mechanic enactment. The only "mechanic" to show is "a large body that hits things," so I depicted the giant winding up an overhead club-smash. Gen 6 is the refined, seed-locked culmination of the strongest direction (the side-profile club-swinger first found in gen 1, raised overhead in gen 3, then cleaned up here). It has the best gestalt of the whole set: an unmistakable hulking-giant silhouette, the boulder-on-a-trunk club hoisted high in both hands mid-swing, a crisp readable head/face, full color and terrain identity (warm red-tan flesh, dry yellow hill grass, grey boulders, sunlit slope), and it reads as "hill giant about to smash" in a single glance at thumbnail size. It beats the dramatic-but-muddy worm's-eye gen 2 (read as a dark demon boss) and edges out the busier gen 4 (muddy face) and the slightly edge-cropped, flatter gen 5.
