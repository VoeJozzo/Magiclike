Ember Anvil is an Artifact Land Mountain that taps for red mana, so the art must enact "a forged object that is also a mountain and produces red heat/mana." This frame nails it: a cleanly legible cast-iron blacksmith anvil in side profile — flat face, distinct horn, waisted body on a stepped stone base — with forge fire rising behind it, molten lava pooling beneath, and glowing red coals lining the base. The anvil silhouette is the most readable and detailed of all seven rolls (critical at 64x32), and the forge-fire-and-lava treatment makes the red mana production literal rather than decorative. It combines the crisp, unmistakable anvil shape of gen 2 with the dramatic full-frame red glow of gen 5, carrying strong red color identity without sacrificing subject clarity. The whole image reads, at a glance, as a working forge-anvil radiating heat — exactly the artifact + Mountain + red mechanic-in-art.
