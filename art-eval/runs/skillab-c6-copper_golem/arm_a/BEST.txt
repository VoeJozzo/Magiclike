Copper Golem is a vanilla {3} colorless 3/3 artifact construct with no abilities, so it is a literal-card: the material IS the art. The single most specific, least-generic thing the name offers is copper's signature transformation — it oxidizes to blue-green verdigris — so I leaned the whole frame on that warm-copper-vs-verdigris two-tone instead of defaulting to a grey robot. Gen 3 is the cleanest realization: a broad, blocky humanoid golem, perfectly centered and full-body, copper-orange plating with verdigris patina pooled in the recesses, a single glowing green core-eye as the focal point (selling "animated construct"), framed sharply against a cold-lit ruined-hall archway with a daylight halo behind it. The silhouette is unmistakable at 64x32 — "a metal golem standing in a doorway" — and the figure pops off the background. It beats the orange-dominant rolls (gen 2, gen 4) on copper-specificity and beats the brighter gen 5 on iconic, uncluttered full-body composition (gen 5 crops the feet). It is the most material-true, most readable frame of the run.
