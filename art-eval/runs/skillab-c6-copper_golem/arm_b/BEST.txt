Copper Golem is a vanilla 3/3 colorless Artifact Creature Construct with no keywords, triggers, or effects — a literal card, not a story card, so the art's whole job is to make the THING read unmistakably as a copper golem with a strong silhouette and correct material identity (copper, not gold/iron). Gen 4 does that best. It is the cleanest, most legible frame in the spread: a single readable hulking-construct silhouette, a warm reddish-copper body with verdigris-teal in the seams (correct oxidized-copper identity, where gen 1 skewed gold and gen 8 skewed red), a glowing cyan eye (a set motif shared with alloy_construct), heavy riveted plating, and a forge-arch background with molten glow that grounds it as a scene rather than a floating sprite and harmonizes with the existing golem_forge / sentinel_colossus construct arts. It was produced by seed-locking gen 1's excellent hulking composition and tweaking only the metal color toward copper — surgical iteration that kept the best silhouette while fixing its one nameable flaw (the gold tone). Gen 7 is the runner-up (most vivid verdigris) but is busier and clips the head; gen 4 wins on overall gestalt.
