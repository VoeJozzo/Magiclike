This frame is the only one that enacts the card's defining mechanic — Archmage Patriarch is a Wizard lord (+1/+1 to your other Wizards), and here he literally stands at the center of his order with two smaller blue-and-gold-robed apprentice wizards flanking him and deferring to him, visually staging the buff he grants. The second beat, the cast-trigger loot, is carried by the large bright sapphire spellbook he holds aloft as the scene's brightest element. Full blue color identity, a real library scene (not a sprite on negative space), and a clean readable silhouette at 64x32: one tall central figure with two small flankers. Gen 3 was a more polished single-figure throne portrait, but it depicts only a generic enthroned blue archmage; Gen 5 is the only frame whose composition says "patriarch OF wizards," which is what makes this card itself.
