Frostbite Mage's effect is "weaken an opponent creature's POWER" (pump -2/0) on arrival, and this frame shows exactly that: a blue frost-wizard (left) blasts a cone of cyan cold at a hulking enemy brute (right) whose raised swinging arm is sheathed in jagged blue ice, his attack arrested mid-strike, the club drooping -- the enemy's power is being visibly sapped, not killed and not merely shot at. Both figures are crisp readable silhouettes; the cyan-on-snow palette nails the blue (U) color identity; and it reads as a scene the mechanic is happening inside, a caster + visibly-affected victim, rather than a wizard posing on negative space. Gen 09 is a close, slightly more polished second on the same concept, and Gen 03 is the single clearest "enemy frozen solid" read -- but Gen 01 best captures the specific power-sap beat with the most dynamic, balanced composition.
