Rationale: Storm Charm is a flash modal RED spell the player casts. Gen 10 is the
cleanest mechanic-in-art for "casting the spell": a hooded battlemage in side profile
thrusts an arm forward and hurls a branching fork of red lightning across a ruined
stone courtyard. The silhouette reads instantly at 64x32 (robed caster anchored right,
luminous forked bolt streaking left), the color identity is unambiguously red, and the
branching bolt nods to the spell's three modes / Lichtenberg fork without becoming an
abstract sky. It is a real scene (architecture + depth), not a sprite on void, so it
fills the frame. Beats Gen 4 (great Frenzy energy but reads as a creature/warrior, not
a spell) and Gen 3 (strong Sweep multi-strike but busier and figure-soup at small size).

Runners-up worth keeping: gen 04 (Frenzy charge, best raw dynamism), gen 03 (Sweep
mode, two clean forks over a line of warriors), gen 05 (the literal charm amulet in hand).
