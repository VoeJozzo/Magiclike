This frame best enacts the card's mechanic: a creature you control surging +3/+3. The wild boar reads as unmistakably swollen and over-muscled, and a bright radiant emerald-green aura of vital growth-energy rises and crackles around its body with thick vines bursting across its enlarged back — the pump is visibly *happening to the creature*, not merely decorated around it, and the rising surge sells the sudden, flash-speed nature of the spell. It is the cleanest fusion of a legible 64x32 silhouette (clear tusked boar head, heavy bulk) with active green-magic enactment, and its palette/forest backdrop sit squarely in the existing green set (forest_titan's mossy bulk, verdant_charm's green vital tendrils). It was reached by seed-locked iteration from gen 1 -> gen 3 (push the energy) -> gen 5 (sharpen the snout/bulk) -> gen 7 (add the bright surging aura), each step targeting a named flaw. Gen 8 (lush vine-mass boar) is a close second; the bear explores (gen 4, 6) lost the green growth-energy to the subject's placid prior and rank below the boar line.
