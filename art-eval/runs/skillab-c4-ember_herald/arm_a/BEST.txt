Ember Herald is a 1/2 red Human Shaman whose only mechanic is an enters-the-battlefield ping: on arrival it deals 1 damage to a creature or player. The art has to enact that arrival-spark striking a target, not just show a shaman standing around. Gen 6 is the cleanest read of the whole batch: the crimson-robed, brass-banded shaman (dominant red color identity, clearly human) thrusts his open palm forward with a bright ember visibly igniting AT his fingertips and a thin flame-lance connecting across the frame to an armored guard at right, who is hunched in a legible defensive recoil, fully visible head to knee and the same size as the caster. You can see the ember leave his hand and hit the victim -- that is the ETB ping, depicted as a moment rather than decorated as a theme. It is a real scene (stone gateway, lava glow at lower-left, dusk), not a sprite floating on void, and the left-to-right composition gives a strong, unambiguous 64x32 silhouette. It edges out gens 3 and 5 (same seed-locked herald-vs-guard composition, also strong) specifically because the spark-at-fingertip and the guard's recoil are both more legible here, making the cause-and-effect of the mechanic the clearest in the set.
