This is the only roll that enacts the card's actual rule rather than just decorating its theme. Soulblade Captain's trigger pumps ALL your creatures +1/+0 whenever you cast another spell, so the art needs the team-wide blade-flare, not a lone hero. Gen 4 delivers exactly that: the captain lunges forward with his soulblade blazing a bright cold-white burst at the tip, and behind and beside him a line of his soldiers raise their own swords, the same white spirit-light racing along every blade in unison -- the moment a spell goes off and the whole squad's weapons sharpen. It reads as a real dawn battlefield scene (not a sprite on negative space), it has the strongest, most legible soul-light glow of any roll, and every figure is clearly visible. Its one flaw is the red cape on a white-aligned card -- a fixable, nameable color note. Per the skill's reach-don't-hedge principle I rank this reaching frame above the cleaner-but-quieter alternatives: gen 3 has tidier white/gold color identity but only implies the squad through dim background shapes, and gen 6's cape recolor landed only by losing the team read entirely. Gen 4 commits to the mechanic.
