This frame is the cleanest mechanic-enactment of Threaten's actual rule: temporarily seizing control of an opponent's creature, untapping it and giving it haste so it turns on its former master. The art shows exactly that transfer of control as a visible mechanism -- a red-caped warlock in side profile thrusts an arm out and a glowing red chain/tether of compulsion links to the war-beast's head, the connection point flaring red as the beast is wrenched toward him, hunched and lunging (the untap/haste read: alert and ready to charge). It is a balanced two-subject scene -- both the warlock and the beast are fully visible head to foot at comparable size, so neither is dropped or swallowed -- with clean, readable silhouettes at 64x32 and strong red color identity matching the card's mana cost. Compared to gen 01 (chain too subtle, figure small), gen 02 (just a scary monster -- no transfer of control shown), and gen 03 (the red force reads as a beam, warlock small), gen 04 has the best gestalt: you instantly read "a sorcerer coercing an enemy beast to his side," which is precisely what the card does.
