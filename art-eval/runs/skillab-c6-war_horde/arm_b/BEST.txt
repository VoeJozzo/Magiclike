War Horde is a literal card, not a story card -- a 4/3 red Goblin Warrior named "Horde." The art's job is to make the COLLECTIVE MASS read at a glance while still giving the eye a focal point, which is hard at 64x32. Gen 8 nails both: one big snarling green goblin leads the charge in the foreground with a clean, unmistakable charging silhouette (the silhouette test winner), and behind him a visibly deep mass of dozens more goblins fans out with raised spears and shields -- so it reads instantly as "a goblin leading a war-horde," not as a lone creature. The bright overcast green-field palette also makes the green goblins pop and gives the frame a fresh look distinct from the default red-instant fire-sky register that several other rolls fell into. Close runners-up: gen 9 (even deeper horde depth but a muddier lead figure) and gen 2 (most cinematic worm's-eye hero + spear-forest, in the busier fiery palette).
