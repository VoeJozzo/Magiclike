Rationale: Best enacts the card's defining mechanic combo — a Wall (unmistakable crenellated marble fortress rampart) that FLIES (great stone-feathered angelic wings spread wide, holding it aloft above clouds), in clean white/colorless identity (pale marble, golden holy dawn light). Strong centered heroic silhouette that reads at 64x32; the spread wings give an instant "flying" signal that a grounded wall would lack (flying is the skill's strong literal-depiction exception). Harmonizes with the set's stone-masonry guardian vocabulary (City Guardian, Wall of Omens). 0/5 pure-defense feel comes through as immovable, sanctified bulwark.

Runner-up: gen_04 (same winged-fortress idea, cooler underside view, murkier) and gen_08 (striking serene marble sentinel face between pillars — a strong alternative "steadfast guardian" direction if the director prefers a portrait over the flying-fortress scene).
