This frame best enacts the card's mechanic. Strength of the Pack is a green combat trick (with Flash) that gives a creature +2/+2 and trample — a sudden surge of strength that lets it crash through. Gen 4 shows a massive gray dire wolf in a hard forward lunge with a brilliant emerald-green surge of growth-energy erupting along its body and trailing behind like a comet. The viewer reads it instantly as "this creature just got a violent burst of green strength," which is exactly the pump-and-Flash beat; the driving forward momentum implies the trample/overrun. It also has the cleanest, most dynamic silhouette of the set, the strongest green color identity (load-bearing on a green card), and the most legible gestalt at 64x32. Gen 6 (vines literally growing along the beast's back) and gen 1 (beefiest body mass) were strong runners-up, but gen 4 reads as the *moment the spell lands* better than any other roll.
