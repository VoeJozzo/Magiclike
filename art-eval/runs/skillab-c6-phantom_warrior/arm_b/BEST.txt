This frame best enacts Phantom Warrior's load-bearing mechanic, unblockable. A solid red-caped armored soldier lunges and thrusts a weapon straight at the translucent cyan phantom on the right, the strike landing on a spectral body that simply cannot be stopped, the very picture of a block that fails. It is the most readable frame in the spread: two clean, distinct, same-size figures, a crisp solid-vs-ghost contrast, full blue color identity for a U creature, and warm-vs-cool tension from the red cape and the torn red banner standing exactly where prompted at the right edge. The moonlit night battlefield harmonizes with the set's existing spirit arts (vengeful_spirit's solid-figure-vs-spectral contrast, echo_spirit's blue ghost-light). It reaches for a specific scene, the strike against the unblockable, and lands it without a glaring flaw, beating the strong but purely decorative solo-portrait rolls (gen 5) and the moodier-but-murkier slipped-past rolls (gen 6, gen 8).
