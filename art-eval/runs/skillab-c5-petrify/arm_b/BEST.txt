This frame is the cleanest enactment of Petrify's mechanic: it turns a living creature into an artifact (stone) until end of turn. The image shows a barbarian warrior caught at the exact instant of transformation — his body is already grey cracked granite, rigid and statue-like (the artifact it has become), while his head and shoulders are still living warm flesh and brown hair, with the calcification visibly creeping up his neck. That flesh-to-stone boundary is the verb of the card made visible: not a finished statue, but a creature mid-petrification. The lunging side-profile gives a strong, readable 64x32 silhouette; the pale golden-white glow on the upper body carries the white color identity and reads as the holy magic doing the petrifying. It reads instantly at size as "a man being turned to stone," which is exactly the rule. Chosen over gen 06 (stone wolf), which is more atmospheric but has a muddier transformation boundary and a warm under-glow that drifts toward a fire/red read.
