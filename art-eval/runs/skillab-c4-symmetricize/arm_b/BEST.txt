Symmetricize forces a target creature into perfect bilateral mirror symmetry, so the art has to SHOW a body being made symmetric — not just a tidy creature. Gen 03 is the frame that enacts the mechanic most legibly: a hulking ogre stands rigid and front-facing while a brilliant vertical blade of white-gold light runs straight down its exact centerline, the visible axis of symmetry that the spell imposes. Both halves read as mirror-balanced around that seam, so the rule ("made symmetric along a central axis") is literally drawn into the composition. It also lands the color identity (a White spell: ordered, radiant gold-white magic in a polished marble colonnade) and passes the 64x32 silhouette test cleanly — one centered creature, a glowing vertical spine of light, framed by columns, sitting in a real scene rather than floating on void. Gen 05 and 06 produced cleaner ogres but lost the bright centerline axis, which is exactly the load-bearing element; gen 02 mirrored harder but read as an abstract Rorschach rather than a single creature; gen 04 was dramatic but rendered too dark/demonic for White. Gen 03 keeps the mechanic visible while staying a believable creature, which is why it wins.
