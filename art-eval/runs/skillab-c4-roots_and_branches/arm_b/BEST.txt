This frame enacts both halves of the card's mechanic in one legible two-figure scene. On the left, an armored warrior is crouched low and clearly entangled — gnarled roots and branches arc over and around him, holding him hunched in place, which reads as the "tap an opponent's creature" (Roots) effect: something bound and immobilized. On the right, a luminous lime-green stag surges with growth, its antlers sprouting like fresh branches and the whole body glowing — that is the "+1/+1 pump on your creature" (Branches) effect made visible. The two halves contrast cleanly: earthy, held-down brown on the left versus radiant green new growth on the right, all in a green-identity forest scene with depth. Across eight rolls this was the only frame where the root-bind read as genuine immobilization rather than scenery, while still nailing the growth-creature, so it best dramatizes "Roots and Branches" doing opposite things to two creatures.
