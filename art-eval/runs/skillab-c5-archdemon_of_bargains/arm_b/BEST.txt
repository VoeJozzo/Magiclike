This frame is the clearest mechanic-enactment of the card: a grim, imposing winged archdemon holding out a glowing parchment contract stamped with visible red wax seals -- the card IS a Faustian bargain (you sign for stickers/boons; the cost transfers to your enemy when it leaves play), and the art shows the demon presenting that sealed pact directly. It nails black color identity (charcoal-black hide + crimson accents + infernal lava glow), the broad bat wings read airborne to honor Flying, the single gold eye and snarl make it menacing rather than cute (fixing the blush-cheek flaw of its parent gen 04), and both the demon and the contract are individually legible at 64x32 with the contract clearly held in-hand. It is a seed-locked tweak of gen 04 that repaired the one named flaw while preserving the strongest composition of the run.
