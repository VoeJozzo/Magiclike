Prey Upon (G sorcery) makes one of your creatures fight an opponent's creature, and its name is a predator/prey image. The art must show TWO creatures locked in a fight, not a single beast. Gen 05 is the only roll that fully delivers that: a dark moss-green predator (the wolf) and a tawny brown boar are face-to-face mid-clash, both fully visible at the same size, snarling with bared fangs and a flash of red blood at the contact point dead-center. The green-vs-brown contrast cleanly separates the two animals so the silhouette reads as a collision of two distinct masses (predator left, prey right) rather than a blob. Color identity is firmly green, the forest background gives it the set's full-bleed-scene look (consistent with feral_stalker / beasts_fury anchors), and the violence is active and legible at 64x32. It beats gen 04 (same composition but more static, less contact energy), gen 01 (predator-over-wreckage, prey muddy), and gen 06 (a beautiful single panther that drops the prey entirely and so fails to enact the two-creature fight). Gen 05 was produced by seed-locking gen 04 and pushing the one variable that mattered — the contact/bite — which sharpened the fight without losing the clean two-animal read.
