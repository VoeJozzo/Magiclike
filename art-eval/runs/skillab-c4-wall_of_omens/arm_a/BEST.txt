This frame is the one that enacts both halves of the card at once. Wall of Omens is a 0/4 defensive Wall that draws a card when it enters — and the card's identity ("omens") says *why* it draws: the wall is a divinatory surface that shows you a glimpse of the future, and that glimpse is the knowledge (card) you gain. Gen 03 reads cleanly at 64x32 as exactly that: a solid grey fieldstone wall filling the frame (the defensive body) with a glowing arched scrying window set into it, and crucially the window contains a *legible omen* — a bright comet/star above a swirling prophetic vision-wisp — rather than an abstract blob or a blank mirror. Among the spread, the warm-gold rolls (05/06) and the clean mirror (07) all lost the actual vision content, collapsing into a featureless glow or a reflective sheen; gen 04 drifted into a round generic magic-orb. Gen 03 keeps the visible omen, the strongest wall-plus-window gestalt, the white-silver light spilling onto the stones, and the moss-slicked base. Its only knock is a cool silver-blue tint that runs a touch off pure white, but that reading actually sells "scrying/prophecy," and keeping the legible omen is worth more than a warmer-but-empty window, since the omen IS the mechanic.
