Symmetricize is a white sorcery that forces a single creature into perfect bilateral symmetry. This frame enacts that mechanic directly: one creature, a white-and-gold armored sentinel, stands dead-center in flawless mirror symmetry with a vertical shaft of radiant white-gold divine light running down its exact midline — the light shaft IS the axis of symmetry being imposed. It is correctly white/gold in color identity (ivory plate, gold trim, grey marble sanctum), the symmetric flanking columns reinforce the order/symmetry theme without creating the "two separate creatures" misread that the mirror-image rolls (gens 1-3) risk, and the strong centered silhouette is highly legible at 64x32. It reaches for specific materials and light rather than generic fantasy filler. Runner-up is gen 03 (a cleanly mirrored bronze horned beast across a bright seam) — a more explicit literal-axis read, but it reads as two creatures and is warm bronze rather than holy white. Gen 06 (golden colossus) is bold and symmetric but skews brass-gold over white and fills the frame so the light-axis nearly vanishes.
