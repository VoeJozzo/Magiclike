Strongest frame of the run and the best mechanic-enactment. Gizzard Beast is a 6/4 trample Beast whose name points at the grinding gut that drives "trample" — overwhelming force that grinds straight through whatever blocks it. This head-on composition puts the viewer squarely in the beast's path: a symmetric green mass fills the frame, both braced forelimbs visible along the bottom edge, two enraged eyes above a wide-gaping maw dead-center showing a red throat and rows of blunt grinding teeth, framed by dark forest trunks left and right. The silhouette is unmistakable at 64x32 (a confrontational beast with a black open-mouth hole as the focal point), the green color identity is clean, and the gestalt reads instantly as "a monster about to grind you up" — trample shown happening to the viewer rather than merely decorated. Gens 02 and 04 land the same maw concept well but at a less confrontational three-quarter angle; Gen 05's dead-on framing and balanced staging make it the pick. (The side-profile trample-the-victim direction in Gens 01/03 produced handsome bodies but the crushed blocker never read legibly — the beast subject-prior kept overriding it.)
