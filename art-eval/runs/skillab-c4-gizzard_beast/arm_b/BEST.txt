This frame best enacts the card. Gizzard Beast is a 6/4 green Trample Beast with no triggers or flavor - a literal card whose only handles are its bulk, its forward-crushing Trample (a force-of-nature character note), and its name: a gizzard is a grinding, devouring gut. Gen 5 delivers a massively muscled, hunched green quadruped in side profile, broad barrel-chested bulk reading as the 6-power body, a gaping fanged grinding maw that sells the devouring-gut name, and a heavy clawed forelimb planted forward in an aggressive trampling lean. The silhouette is unmistakable at 64x32 - a single heavy beast shape - and it sits in a real forest scene framed by two foreground trunks with undergrowth at the base, not a sprite floating on negative space. It out-massed the other strong rolls (gen 2 and gen 3 were cleaner but flatter/leaner); gen 5's musculature and menacing maw give the most legible, most on-name frame.
