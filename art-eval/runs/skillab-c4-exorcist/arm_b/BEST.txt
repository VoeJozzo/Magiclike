Best enacts the card's mechanic. Exorcist's ETB exiles a target creature and gains life equal to its power, so the art needs to show a creature being banished into nothing (exile leaves no corpse) and life-light returning to the cleric. This frame is the clearest read of that: a white-and-gold-robed human cleric in side profile (clean white color identity) faces a large crimson horned demon whose lower body has visibly dissolved into a column of white-gold light and rising motes from the chapel floor — the creature is mid-vanish, no body left behind, exactly the exile beat. Both figures are fully visible and the same size, silhouettes read at 64x32, and the warm radiance doubles as the gain-life glow. Gen 5 and 7 are more dramatic but keep the demon more intact (less "being unmade"); gen 4 is the one where the act of exiling is literally happening on screen.
